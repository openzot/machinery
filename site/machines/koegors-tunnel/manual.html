OPERATING MANUAL
KOEGORS CROSSING TUNNEL No. 1

VENTILATION · DRAINAGE · SIGNALS · INCIDENT CONTROL
PLANT ROOM NOORD · THIRD EDITION · MARCH 1983

This manual is to be kept in the rack beside the desk. Read it before you take over a shift and again after every incident. The bore forgives nobody twice.

Overview

The Koegors Crossing carries the N-49 motorway beneath the Koegors shipping canal. The tunnel is 720 metres long, falls to a low point sump of 240 m³ at chainage 0+360, and is ventilated longitudinally by eight caged jet fans in four ceiling groups, A through D, each group of two jets worth roughly one metre per second of bore air speed. The desk in plant room Noord operates everything: ventilation, portal signals and barriers, bore lighting zones, drainage pumps and — when it all goes wrong — the smoke control key.

Air is moved in the direction of travel. Exhaust haze from traffic is carried out of the north portal by piston effect and the jets; carbon monoxide is kept below 70 ppm and the optical extinction below 9 km⁻¹ by staging fan groups automatically. When a vehicle burns, the law of the road reverses: air must be pushed AWAY from the queue at the southern portal, back through the bore, so the smoke goes out where nobody is standing. The smoke control key does this in one motion.

If an alarm is left unacknowledged and the air keeps fouling — CO above 150 ppm or extinction above 18 km⁻¹ held forty-five seconds — the desk closes the tunnel by itself: signals to red, barriers down, queue held on the approach. It does this so that you do not have to think about it while you are thinking about the fire.

Controls

DESK SUPPLY
Keyed two-position rotary, OFF / ON. Energises the bench, instruments and annunciator. Signals and barriers are battery backed and work without it.
VENTILATION MODE
Four-position rotary. OFF: all jets stopped. AUTO: groups whose paddle sits at AUTO follow the staging law against CO and extinction. HAND: paddles command directly; the law stands down. TEST: same as HAND but with the alarm horn cut, for proving fans one at a time.
FAN GROUP A · FAN GROUP B · FAN GROUP C · FAN GROUP D
Four notched paddles, REVERSE – AUTO – NORMAL. NORMAL forces the group to blow towards Noord, REVERSE towards Zuid, AUTO hands the group to the law; the detent spring returns nothing — set what you mean.
TRIP RESET
Pushbutton. Restores a tripped jet motor once its bearing has cooled below 95 °C. It will not reset a hot trip; wait, watch the bearing strip on the vent status line, then press.
SUMP PUMPS
Three-position rotary OFF / AUTO / HAND. AUTO starts pump P1 at 65 % level and adds P2 above 80 %, stopping both at 22 %. On mains failure a standby feed runs P1 alone.
SUMP HAND RUN
Momentary mushroom, press and hold. Runs P1 (and P2 above 70 %) regardless of mode.
DRAIN FLUSH VALVE
Momentary valve, press and hold. Flushes the collector channels into the sump — use it weekly, and after any spillage, to prove the drains.
PORTAL SIGNAL LEVER
PORTAL SIGNAL LEVER
Three-stop quadrant lever. GREEN: portals open, 3600 veh/h. AMBER: single file, 1800 veh/h. RED: portals shut, barriers down. The lever is overridden to RED during automatic closure until the incident is cleared and accepted.
LIGHTING ZONES
Four guarded rockers, Z1–Z4. Emergency feed carries Zone 2 only; light accordingly when the mains are gone.
SMOKE CONTROL KEY
Red-collared keyswitch under a lifting guard, marked ROOKBESTRIJDING. Lift the guard, turn the key: every running jet reverses to full stage and the purge begins. Return the key to normal to hand the bore back to the law.
FAULT TEST SWITCHES
Hinged steel lid, three switches beneath: FAN SEIZURE, MAINS FAIL, FIRE IN BORE. For weekly proving and for training. Lifting the lid is logged.
ALARM ACCEPT
Pushbutton. Steadies every flashing window. An alarm that is accepted but still present stays lit steady.
HORN CUT
Pushbutton. Silences the klaxon. The next new alarm sounds again.
LAMP TEST
Pushbutton, latching. Lights every annunciator window. Press again to release.
DESK RESET
Pushbutton. Returns the whole installation to cold-and-clear: supply on, ventilation off, paddles at AUTO, signals green, alarms extinguished. Use after an incident has been fully recovered, never as a substitute for recovering one.

Normal operation

  1. On arrival, read the log, then prove the panel: press LAMP TEST and release, and satisfy yourself that every window lights and none is stuck.
  2. Turn DESK SUPPLY to ON. The drums, needles and mimic should wake.
  3. Set LIGHTING ZONES to the daylight conditions — normally all four on at night, Z3–Z4 off in bright weather.
  4. Set SUMP PUMPS to AUTO and confirm STANDBY shows on the duty lamp. Hold DRAIN FLUSH VALVE briefly on Mondays.
  5. Set VENTILATION MODE to AUTO. With clean air the law holds one or two groups; watch LAW STAGE appear on the airflow ribbon.
  6. Check PORTAL SIGNAL LEVER at GREEN and the barriers raised.
  7. Note the CO drum, extinction drum and both recorder pens. CO should sit between 15 and 45 ppm at the morning rush; extinction under 7 km⁻¹.
  8. Walk your eyes along the mimic: eight fans, four zone strips, both signal heads, sump below half. That is a healthy bore. Log the take-over and stop reading this page.

Alarms

Window Meaning Action
CO HIGH Carbon monoxide at or above 70 ppm. Confirm AUTO staging; if the law is already at four groups, look for a trip or a fire. Accept and watch the trend on the red pen.
VIS POOR Extinction at or above 9 km⁻¹ — smoke or heavy diesel haze. As CO HIGH. If drivers report smoke, treat as fire.
AIRFLOW LOW Fans demanded but bore velocity under 0·6 m/s for twenty seconds. Check FAN TRIP and POWER FAIL first; then check no paddle has been left at REVERSE against the law.
FAN TRIP A jet motor protection has operated, usually bearing heat. Note which group shows dark on the mimic. Allow cooling below 95 °C, then TRIP RESET and return the group to service.
SUMP HIGH Level at or above 85 %. Set SUMP PUMPS to HAND and hold SUMP HAND RUN. If the level still climbs, a float or a pump has failed — send a man down with the lift, not a broom.
POWER FAIL Mains lost. Jets stopped, emergency feed carries signals, Zone 2 and standby pump P1. Expect CO to climb slowly on piston effect only. Follow the mains failure procedure below.
FIRE DETECTED Fire in the bore, or smoke lingering after one. Turn the SMOKE CONTROL KEY. Now. Then follow the fire procedure below.
CLOSURE The desk has closed the tunnel: signals red, barriers down. This follows ignored CO or smoke. Do not fight it — fight the cause. When CO is under 110 ppm, extinction under 13 and the cause is handled, accept and the desk reopens.

Faults and recovery

JET FAN SEIZURE

You see: group B dark on the mimic, its bearing figure climbing fast on the vent status strip, then FAN TRIP. A bound spindle cooks whether or not it is driven — piston draught alone will ruin it.

Recovery: put the FAN GROUP B paddle to OFF so nobody restarts it into a wreck. Let the bearing cool below 95 °C — watch the strip, five minutes or so. Press TRIP RESET. Lift the FAULT TEST SWITCHES lid and drop the FAN SEIZURE switch (or have the fitter free the spindle). Return the paddle to AUTO. The law restages the group within seconds.

MAINS SUPPLY FAILURE

You see: POWER FAIL immediately, every jet stops, the mimic dims to Zone 2, the standby pump takes the sump. CO and extinction creep up on piston effect alone.

Recovery: confirm the signals are showing and the barriers behave — they are on batteries. Drop the MAINS FAIL switch under the FAULT TEST SWITCHES lid when the supply is proven restored. Set VENTILATION MODE to AUTO and let the law catch up; if the air is already foul it will run all four groups. Accept the alarms when the pens fall.

VEHICLE FIRE IN BORE

You see: FIRE DETECTED within seconds, the flame mark on the mimic near group C, extinction climbing fast, CO behind it. Ignore it and the desk closes the tunnel at the forty-five second mark; keep ignoring it and the bore fills.

Recovery: lift the guard and turn the SMOKE CONTROL KEY — all jets reverse to full stage and drive the smoke back to the empty Zuid portal. Leave the key turned until the load has burned itself out (a van load of tyres takes six minutes or so; the temperature needle tells you when it is over). When the glow dies and the pens fall, return the key to normal, drop the FIRE IN BORE switch, accept the alarms, and let the law clear the last of it. DESK RESET only when the fire brigade says so.
