Overview

This bench governs the four rapid gravity filters in the south gallery of the Deeping works. River water drawn from above the weir passes downwards through each bed — half a metre of shingle, a metre of Leighton Buzzard sand and a gravel support layer — and leaves by the underdrains as clarified water. It is dosed with chlorine on its way to the covered clear well, and from the well the booster pumps serve the town.

The bench is worked by one filter attendant. His business is a running fight against two enemies: the silting of his beds, and the escape of his chlorine. A bed in duty blinds steadily as it holds back the river's grit; its loss of head, shown on the inclined manometer beside each bed, climbs until the bed must come out of service and be washed. While one bed washes, the other three carry the town. Should the water leave the gallery turbid, or reach the well without its residual of chlorine, the public health is at risk and this bench says so plainly. Do not argue with it.

Starts a wash only when one bed stands isolated with its waste open; a wind against nothing brings a short buzz.
BLOWER START Guarded pushbutton, alternate action Starts and stops the air-scour blower. The guard must be lifted to press. Must be running through the AIR SCOUR stage.
BLOWER RESET Pushbutton Clears a blower overload trip and releases the wash interlock.
CHLORINE DOSE Pointer knob, 0–6 mg/l by halves Sets the dose the ejector tries to carry against the flow. The honest working band is 1½ to 3½ mg/l set-point; watch the comparator, not the knob.
EJECTOR SELECTOR Two-position rotary, DUTY / STBY Selects which solution ejector carries the chlorine vacuum. Turn to STBY when the duty ejector loses vacuum.
RATE LEVER Notched quadrant, 60 / 80 / 100 / 120 % Gallery output against the town demand. Raise it when the wells fall; ease it in spate.
ALARM ACCEPT Pushbutton Stops the flashing of accepted windows; they burn steady until the condition clears.
LAMPS TEST Pushbutton, spring return Lights every annunciator window and jewel for two seconds. Test at the start of every shift.
FAULT TEST SWITCHES Covered test panel, three latching buttons Fitters' proof-of-alarm tests: bed breakthrough, ejector vacuum loss, blower overload. Lift the lid. A lit dot shows the test latched.
GALLERY RESET Mushroom pushbutton on brass collar Returns the whole bench to cold: wheels shut, cocks shut, faults cleared, alarms dark. Used after SUPPLY OFF or PUBLIC NOTICE, and to hand over a quiet bench.

Normal operation

  1. On arrival, press LAMPS TEST. Every window and jewel must light. Press ALARM ACCEPT and satisfy yourself the board is dark.
  2. Note the works clock, the clear well level and the raw turbidity on the river dial. If the river is in spate, set the RATE LEVER to 60 % before anything else.
  3. Open each bed to duty: turn BED 1 INLET, BED 1 OUTLET, and likewise for beds 2, 3 and 4, to two turns or more apiece. Confirm each bed's DUTY jewel burns and its manometer begins to climb off zero.
  4. Set the RATE LEVER to 80 % and compare OUTPUT with TOWN DEMAND on the twin dials at the clear well. If the well is below half, take 100 %.
  5. Set CHLORINE DOSE to 3 mg/l and confirm the EJECTOR SELECTOR stands at DUTY and the vacuum gauge pulls above fifteen inches.
  6. Watch the residual comparator settle between the 0·2 and 0·3 tints. Trim the dose by halves until it does.
  7. Walk the gallery once an hour: manometers rising together mean ordinary silting; one out of step means trouble in that bed.
  8. When a bed's loss of head passes two metres, isolate it — both wheels shut, waste cock OPEN — and hold the WASH TIMER CRANK through DRAIN, AIR SCOUR (blower running), WATER WASH and REFILL. At the gong, shut the waste cock and bring the bed back on the wheels.
  9. Hand over with the board dark or accepted, the comparator in tint, and the well above sixty per cent.

Alarms

Window Meaning Action
HIGH LOSS OF HEAD A duty bed is passing two and a half metres. It will crack and pass dirt shortly. Isolate and wash that bed at once; watch the remaining three carry the town.
TURBIDITY BREAKTHROUGH Water is leaving a bed dirty — a cracked bed or a sand hole. Identify the clouded sight-glass, isolate that bed and give it a full wash.
CHLORINE RESIDUAL LOW The comparator reads under 0·15 mg/l: no dose reaching the water. Check the ejector vacuum. If the DUTY line has lost vacuum, change the EJECTOR SELECTOR to STBY.
CLEAR WELL LOW The well is under a fifth full. Raise the RATE LEVER; close any wash in progress. If she falls further the outlet trips — see SUPPLY OFF.
BACKWASH FAULT The air-scour blower has tripped during a scour. Press BLOWER RESET, then give the crank half a turn to release the interlock.
RAW WATER SPATE The river is carrying more than nine NTU. Ease the RATE LEVER to 60 % and expect to wash beds sooner.
SUPPLY OFF The well emptied and the outlet has tripped to protect the boosters. The town is unserved until you restore. Bring production back above demand, and when the well passes thirty per cent press GALLERY RESET and restart by the Normal operation procedure.
PUBLIC NOTICE Bad water has been served for over two minutes: turbid or without chlorine. This window will not go out while the record stands. Cure the cause, then press GALLERY RESET. The Medical Officer decides on any notice to boil; expect a telephone call either way.

Faults and recovery

Filter bed breakthrough

You see: the bed's sight-glass clouds to the colour of the river while its manometer may look unremarkable; TURBIDITY BREAKTHROUGH flashes; a test dot lights in the FAULT TEST SWITCHES lid.

Recovery: isolate the affected bed — shut its inlet and outlet wheels, open its waste cock — and put it through a complete wash: DRAIN, AIR SCOUR, WATER WASH, REFILL. A full wash re-levels the sand and closes the hole; the alarm clears as the bed settles. Merely opening and shutting the wheels will not mend a broken bed.

Chlorine ejector vacuum loss

You see: the ejector vacuum gauge sagging towards zero, the comparator sample fading to the left, CHLORINE RESIDUAL LOW flashing. The duty solution line has given up its vacuum and no chlorine is being carried.

Recovery: turn the EJECTOR SELECTOR to STBY. Vacuum rebuilds inside half a minute and dosing resumes on the standby line; the alarm clears when the comparator stands above 0·15 mg/l. If both lines fail together, close the CHLORINE DOSE to zero, raise nothing, and telephone the chlorination plant attendant — do not serve water without residual.

Backwash blower overload

You see: the blower jewel goes out mid-scour, the sequence holds at AIR SCOUR, BACKWASH FAULT flashes. The motor thermal overload has tripped.

Recovery: press BLOWER RESET — the jewel relights — then give the WASH TIMER CRANK half a turn to release the interlock, and let the scour finish. If the overload trips again, let the motor cool twenty minutes before a second attempt, and finish the wash on water alone by holding the crank through to WASH.
