Thurlby Fields Aerodrome — Ground-Lighting Regulator Mk. 2
four 6.6 A series circuits · thyristor regulators · standby alternator

Return to the desk Machine catalogue

Overview

This desk switches and regulates every airfield ground light at Thurlby Fields: the approach centreline barrettes, the threshold wing bars, the runway edge and the approach path indicator. Each group of lamps hangs on its own series loop fed by a thyristor constant-current regulator; the regulator holds the loop current at one of five fixed steps whatever the lamp load, and the intensity you select is therefore a current, not a dimmer setting. The field itself is shown under the smoked hood in the middle of the desk. Judge colour there, with the room lights down — never by leaning out of the window.

Mains comes from the aerodrome ring main; if it fails, a 40 kVA diesel alternator picks the bus up automatically in about two seconds. The alternator is smaller than the mains, and it will sag if you demand crash-max from it. Treat it as a torch, not a power station.

Foster & Braithwaite Ltd, Reading — works № 1184 — commissioned 4 May 1974. Amendments are taped on the works-test panel; do not remove them.

Controls

Control Type Action and positions
APPROACH INTENSITY Rotary selector OFF, then steps 1–5 for the centreline barrettes (48, 64, 77, 89, 100 % of 6.6 A). Click right half to advance, left half to ease; arrow keys work the same.
THRESHOLD INTENSITY Rotary selector OFF, steps 1–5 for the threshold bar and wing bars.
EDGE INTENSITY Rotary selector OFF, steps 1–5 for the runway edge loop.
PAPI INTENSITY Rotary selector OFF, steps 1–5 for the approach path indicator. Step 5 is the survey setting; colour grading is done at it.
APPROACH ISOLATOR Rotary selector NORMAL, or BYPASS A–D. Each position bridges one quarter of the centreline loop round a damaged section. Used to clear an earth fault; see Faults and recovery.
REGULATOR BLOWERS Bat toggle Starts the regulator bay fans. Any circuit above about half current without airflow will overheat and trip.
CRASH MAX SWITCH Guarded bat toggle Under the red lift-up guard. Throws all four selectors to step 5 regardless of where they stood; lowering it restores what was set before. For an emergency only — see Alarms.
ALARM ACCEPT Pushbutton Stops the flash and the horn on a new alarm; the window stays lit while the cause remains.
ALARM RESET Pushbutton Extinguishes windows whose cause has cleared, and re-closes a tripped regulator once its temperature is below 88 °C.
LAMP TEST Pushbutton Proves every filament, neon and annunciation window at full brilliance for about a second and a half.
INSULATION TEST Pushbutton Reads the approach-loop insulation onto the kΩ reel. A healthy loop holds 999 kΩ; a damaged one reads tens or single digits.
WORKS TEST PANEL Recessed test bay Three spring-return buttons that imitate the three standing faults — BARRETTE FAIL, BLOWER-side thermal runaway (OVERHEAT), and MAINS FAIL — plus the COLD START key and the INSULATION TEST button. Engineering use only; log every press.
COLD START KEY Key switch Turns the whole desk cold and alarm-free: selectors to OFF, fans off, guard state kept, counters zeroed after lamp replacement.

Normal operation

  1. Confirm the MAINS ON pilot is lit and the bus voltmeter reads 240 V (nothing below 235 V).
  2. Switch REGULATOR BLOWERS ON and leave it on for the watch.
  3. Set APPROACH INTENSITY to step 3. The approach ammeter settles on the amber 77 % arc inside a few seconds.
  4. Set THRESHOLD INTENSITY to step 3 (77 % arc).
  5. Set EDGE INTENSITY to step 4 (amber 89 % arc). With the fans running the COOLING AIR window stays dark.
  6. Set PAPI INTENSITY to step 5 — the red 100 % arc. Confirm the PAPI head shows two white over two red.
  7. Look under the hood: green threshold bar and wing bars, white centreline with the sequenced flasher running toward the threshold, white edge, red end lights.
  8. Read the lamp-out counters (zero on a good night), the insulation reel (999 kΩ) and the annunciator (all windows dark). The desk is now on watch; temperatures should sit between 40 and 70 °C.

Do not raise the crash guard except on the crash bell. Crash-max draws the four loops at full current together; on the standby alternator it will drag the bus below 208 volts and the desk will begin shedding circuits for you.

Alarms

Window Means Do
LAMP FAILURE Filaments are out and the counters have counted them. With EARTH FAULT it is a damaged section, not old lamps. Note the counters; see Faults and recovery if EARTH FAULT shows.
EARTH FAULT Approach-loop insulation has fallen (INSULATION TEST reads single digits instead of 999 kΩ). Fit the correct bypass on APPROACH ISOLATOR, then ALARM RESET.
OVERTEMP A regulator winding has passed 95 °C. Ease the steps and confirm the blowers run; a trip follows at 105 °C.
REGULATOR TRIP The thermal contactor has opened a loop. Its lamps are dark. Find why (OVERTEMP, LOW VOLTAGE), let the strip fall below 88 °C, then ALARM RESET to re-close.
COOLING AIR More than half current flowing with the fan bank off. Switch REGULATOR BLOWERS on. Do not ignore it: the next window is OVERTEMP.
STANDBY SUPPLY Mains lost; the alternator is carrying the field. Ease every selector one step. Expect the bus to sag if you do not.
LOW VOLTAGE Bus below 208 V — overload on the alternator. Ease steps at once. Sustained sag trips the heaviest circuit after about twelve seconds.

New alarms flash and sound the horn until ALARM ACCEPT is pressed; accepted alarms burn steady until the cause clears and ALARM RESET is pressed. LAMP FAILURE alone (counters moving, insulation healthy) is a maintenance note, not a night-stoppage: fly on.

Faults and recovery

approach barrette failure

You see: two adjacent centreline barrettes dark under the hood, LAMP FAILURE and EARTH FAULT flashing together, the insulation reel falling to 008 kΩ on test, the approach ammeter sitting below its arc.

To clear: rotate APPROACH ISOLATOR from NORMAL through BYPASS A, B, C, D — watching the EARTH FAULT window. When you pass the position that bridges the damaged quarter, the window stops flashing and the rest of the centreline relights. Press ALARM RESET. The failed barrettes stay dark until the lamps are changed at servicing; the counters keep the figure. COLD START after the lamp van returns the desk to new.

regulator overheat

You see: the edge regulator's temperature strip climbing quickly whatever the load — a thyristor has welded through and is heating the stack from inside. OVERTEMP flashes around 95 °C; at 105 °C the REGULATOR TRIP window follows and the edge loop goes dark.

To clear: nothing at the desk cures a welded stack — easing the selector will not help. Let it trip. When the strip has fallen below 88 °C, ALARM RESET re-closes the contactor and the lamps return, but the climb will begin again: that is the fault repeating itself, not your handling. Keep the re-close for essential movements, note the time in the log, and telephone the contractor. The COLD START key returns the desk cold with the condition cleared for the bench test.

mains supply failure

You see: the voltmeter sagging to nothing, the MAINS ON pilot out, STANDBY SUPPLY flashing; about two seconds later the STANDBY RUN pilot lights as the alternator picks up the bus.

To clear: nothing you do restores the mains — ease every selector one step and carry on reduced. If crash-max was held through the changeover, LOW VOLTAGE flashes and the desk sheds its heaviest circuit after some seconds; let it, then ALARM RESET and rebuild gently. When the mains returns the MAINS ON pilot relights; ALARM RESET extinguishes the standby windows. COLD START resets the desk after a full drill.

Specifications

Supply 240 V, 50 Hz, single phase, aerodrome ring main
Standby supply 40 kVA diesel alternator, automatic pickup ≈ 2 s, sustained sag below 208 V sheds load
Circuits 4 series loops: approach centreline, threshold, runway edge, PAPI
Regulators F/B·CCR·4 thyristor constant-current, 6.6 A full rating
Intensity steps 48 / 64 / 77 / 89 / 100 % of rated current
Thermal trip 105 °C winding, re-close permitted below 88 °C
Earth monitoring Injection type, alarmed below 50 kΩ; damaged loop typically reads ≈ 8 kΩ
Centreline 14 barrettes × 5 lamps, cross type, 60 m spacing, sequenced flasher fitted
Threshold 6-lume bar and twin 5-lamp wing bars, green
Edge 15 pairs elevated white, 60 m spacing
PAPI 4-unit, survey setting at step 5, on-grade 3.0°, two-white-two-red band 2.8°–3.2°
Mimic hood Smoked acrylic, hinge at top; judge colour with hood down
