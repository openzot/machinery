Overview

Pile No. 2 is a zero-energy, pool-type research reactor. Twenty-eight enriched-uranium aluminium-alloy elements hang in an open aluminium tank of light water; the water moderates, reflects and cools. The pile is rated 10 kW of heat, and is worked far below that for most experiments — its business is a steady, measurable neutron flux, not power.

The start-up console puts the whole plant in front of one operator. On the left, the counting chain: a boron-trifluoride counter on channel A or B, its log count-rate meter, period meter and the neon decades. In the middle, the sectioned mimic of the tank and the shim-rod drive — a braked handwheel through a magnetic clutch, with the regulating-rod vernier beside it. On the right, heat removal: fuel and bulk-water thermometers, the loop-flow sight glass and the cooling valve. Below everything, within reach of both hands, the red scram bar.

The pile has no dangerous habits if it is brought up slowly. It has one unforgiving one: a fast doubling. Keep the period long, keep the fuel cool, and the pile will sit at criticality all afternoon like a well-set watch.

Controls

Control Type Action
MAINS BREAKER guarded toggle Energises the console, drive motor, pumps and counting chain. Guard raised to operate.
LAMPS TEST mushroom button, hold Holds every annunciator window and decade neon alight while pressed.
ALARM ACCEPT mushroom button Stops the flash of acknowledged alarms; they burn steady until the condition clears.

DETECTOR HT guarded toggle High tension to the selected counter. OFF / HT ON.
MODE SELECTOR rotary, 3 positions SAFE drives the shim rod home. MANUAL frees the handwheel. AUTO engages the regulator, which holds 5 kW on the regulating rod.
SHIM ROD HANDWHEEL spoked handwheel Withdraws (anti-clockwise, drag up) or inserts the shim rod. Turns free when the clutch is OUT.

REGULATING CRANK notched crank Trims the regulating rod ±120 pcm for fine criticality. The regulator owns it in AUTO.

SCRAM BAR full-width red bar Strikes the trip relay: shim rod dumps home by spring in 1.1 s. Works even on a dead panel.
TRIP RESET red button Clears a scram once power is under 2 kW and fuel temperature under 92°C.
COLD RESET button Returns the whole panel to cold and alarm-free without re-energising anything.
MAINTENANCE TESTS three guarded test toggles Engineers only. Marked T simulate EHT failure, clutch slip and flow loss; restore to N after test and follow §5.

Normal operation

  1. Walk the panel. Confirm MODE SELECTOR at SAFE, DRIVE CLUTCH OUT, DETECTOR SELECTOR OFF, COOLING VALVE SHUT, SCRAM BAR latched and un-struck.
  2. Raise the guard and put the MAINS BREAKER ON. The supervisory strip lights; the period meter falls to rest. Press LAMPS TEST briefly and satisfy yourself that every window burns.
  3. Put the CHART MOTOR to RUN. Sign the chart edge with date and shift.
  4. Turn the DETECTOR SELECTOR to A, then raise the DETECTOR HT guard and switch HT ON. Source counts of twenty to thirty per second should stand on the log meter — the pile is asleep, not dead. If the meter stays dead, see §5, detector high-voltage failure.
  5. Turn the MODE SELECTOR to MANUAL. Engage the DRIVE CLUTCH lever IN.
  6. Withdraw the shim rod with the handwheel in stages of ten percent, pausing at each stage until the period needle settles back toward infinity and the CRITICAL eye stays dark. Never let the period needle enter the red sector: below twenty seconds, stop withdrawing; below eight seconds the trip will do it for you.
  7. Watch the REACTIVITY meter as you work. As it swings positive of +50 pcm the count-rate will climb steadily — approach criticality in smaller steps now.
  8. On the first steady rise past one kilowatt, open the COOLING VALVE to about sixty percent. Check the loop flow pellet lifts and FUEL TEMP settles near fifty-five degrees.
  9. Pass through criticality: the CRITICAL lamp lights when the reactivity meter crosses zero with power stable. Trim about the point with the REGULATING CRANK, a notch at a time.
  10. For a held level, turn the MODE SELECTOR to AUTO. The regulator takes the vernier and holds 5 kW. Log the run and let the chart tell the story.
  11. To shut down: strike the SCRAM BAR, or take MODE to SAFE and let the rod drive home. When power is under two kilowatts and fuel temperature under ninety-two, press TRIP RESET, close the cooling valve, switch HT OFF, CHART OFF, and leave the MAINS BREAKER ON only if the next shift follows at once.

Alarms

Window Means Do
SOURCE The selected counting chain has lost high tension; you are flying blind. Select detector channel B (EHT unit no. 2). If neither chain lives, shut down and send for instruments.
PERIOD Doubling time under twenty seconds. The pile is running away from you. Stop withdrawing. Hold the wheel or ease it in until the needle recovers past twenty. Under eight seconds the trip scrams by itself.
FUEL TEMP Element temperature above 82°C; above 97°C the pile trips itself and flags possible element damage. Open the COOLING VALVE. Reduce power. After a temperature trip, inspect per §5 before the next start-up.
FLOW Loop circulation lost or starved with power on the pile. Open the COOLING VALVE; if flow does not return, reduce power immediately and treat as §5, coolant flow loss.
DRIVE The drive screw is slipping; the shim rod is creeping home uncommanded. Drop the clutch OUT to reset the screw, re-engage IN, and re-drive to level. See §5.
SCRAM Trip relay struck — bar, short period, power excursion or fuel temperature. Let the rod lie in. Find the reason. Clear with TRIP RESET only when power is under two kilowatts and fuel under ninety-two degrees.

A flashing window is unacknowledged. ALARM ACCEPT stops the flash but never the condition; the window burns steady until the fault is actually cleared.

Faults and recovery

Detector high-voltage failure

You see: the count-rate meter collapses toward background whatever the pile is doing; inside a few seconds the SOURCE window flashes. Power readings from the heat side are your only truth meanwhile — if fuel temperature is still climbing, the pile is not shutting itself down.

Clear it: turn the DETECTOR SELECTOR to B, which hands the counter to spare EHT unit no. 2. Counts return at once and SOURCE extinguishes when accepted. Channel A stays out of service until the instrument section changes the tube; COLD RESET clears the simulated fault for training runs.

Control-rod clutch slip

You see: the DRIVE window flashes and the shim rod creeps inward at about two percent a second though nobody is turning the wheel — power droops, reactivity falls.

Clear it: throw the DRIVE CLUTCH lever OUT; this resets the slipped screw. Engage IN again and drive back to level with the handwheel, watching the period needle as always. If the slip returns twice in one shift, tag the drive OUT OF SERVICE and use SAFE mode.

Coolant flow loss

You see: the FLOW window flashes, the sight-glass pellet drops, and fuel temperature begins a slow climb that negative feedback only partly checks. Ignored above ninety-seven degrees the pile trips itself, latches FUEL TEMP, and the run is written off pending inspection.

Clear it: open the COOLING VALVE fully — past eighty-five percent the hard opening resets the circulator contactor — and confirm the pellet lifts. Ease back to sixty percent. After any genuine flow loss, verify fuel temperature falling before resuming power.

Specifications

Reactor
Type Pool-type thermal reactor, light-water moderated and reflected
Fuel 28 elements, uranium-aluminium alloy, 20% enriched
Rated power 10 kW (thermal); normal working level 5 kW
Neutron source Ra-Be, 0.05 W equivalent, fixed in reflector
Reactivity
Shim rod worth 900 pcm, full travel, spring-dump 1.1 s

Protection

Alarm annunciation SOURCE, PERIOD, FUEL TEMP, FLOW, DRIVE, SCRAM
Instrumentation
Counting channels BF₃ counters A and B, EHT units 1 and 2, log scale 1–10⁶ cps

Heat removal

Bulk water temperature 22°C nominal, 40°C limit
