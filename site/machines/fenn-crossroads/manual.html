OPERATING MANUAL
AUTOMATIC SIGNAL CONTROLLER MK III

AS FITTED AT THE FENN CROSSROADS JUNCTION, FENN RD. × CARROW LN.
CABINET TYPE M3/240 — KEEP THIS BOOK IN THE CABINET POCKET

Overview

The Mk III controller works the traffic signals at the Fenn Crossroads entirely by machinery. A synchronous motor, fed from the mains through the MAINS ISOLATOR, drives a reduction gear and a camshaft; three adjustable cams — the MAIN GREEN CAM, the SIDE GREEN CAM and the WALK CAM — open and close mercury switches in strict order and so step the junction through its intervals. Vehicle treadles laid across each approach register demand calls on the ratchet counters beside the mechanism, and a conflict monitor watches the lamps themselves.

If a filament fails, or if traffic stands beyond the capacity of the timing for ninety seconds together, the monitor drops the junction on to alternating red flash, lights the ON TRANSFER pilot and rings the striker bell until it is answered. The delivered condition of the plant — and the condition reached by pressing PLANT RESET — is supply on, selector at AUTO, timings 30 / 20 / 8 seconds, counters at zero and no alarms standing.

Controls

Control Positions Duty
MAINS ISOLATOR OFF / ON Connects or disconnects the mains supply to the whole cabinet. Isolate before touching a lamp.
MODE SELECTOR FLASH / AUTO / HAND Picks alternating red flash, ordinary automatic working, or hand control of every interval.
INTERVAL CRANK wound / stowed Winds the camshaft forward by hand while the selector stands at HAND. Hold it turned; the shaft follows the crank.
MAIN GREEN CAM 15–60 s, 5 s steps Sets the length of the Fenn Road green. Pull the knurled clutch knob towards you, turn the drum to the wanted mark, release to re-engage.
SIDE GREEN CAM 10–40 s, 5 s steps Sets the length of the Carrow Lane green by the same pull-and-turn.
WALK CAM 5–15 s, 1 s steps Sets how long the pedestrian WALK lamp burns inside the Carrow Lane green. Never more than side green less three seconds.
POLICE PUSHBUTTON momentary In HAND working, steps the junction to the start of the next interval. For police use at point duty.
EMERGENCY FLASH KEY vertical / turned Put the junction on alternating red flash on the word of police or fire, whatever the selector reads.
TREADLE TEST press Registers one artificial Carrow Lane call to prove detectors, counters and change of stage. A press also frees a sticking treadle iron.
LAMP RESTORE press Re-arms the conflict monitor after a lamp has been replaced and releases the forced flash.
BELL CUT in / out Silences the striker bell while an alarm is attended. Leave out (armed) at all other times.
MAINTENANCE TESTS guarded LAMP FAIL, CLUTCH SLIP, TREADLE STICK Works-test switches under hinged guards. They reproduce the three recognised faults for instruction; leave all down and guarded after use.
PLANT RESET press Returns the controller to its delivered condition: running, timed 30 / 20 / 8, alarms cleared, counters at zero.

Normal operation

  1. Press PLANT RESET to bring the works to its delivered condition, or begin from a cabinet just energised: the POWER jewel should be lit, the voltmeter should stand between 230 and 250 volts, and the flywheel should be turning steadily.
  2. Confirm the MODE SELECTOR stands at AUTO. The interval jewels should read, in order, MAIN GREEN, MAIN AMBER, ALL RED, SIDE GREEN (or WALK), SIDE AMBER, ALL RED, and back to MAIN GREEN.
  3. Watch one full revolution of the CYCLE TIMER needle against the coloured sectors. The amber sector should span the MAIN GREEN CAM setting, the green sector the SIDE GREEN CAM setting, counting the scale in seconds.
  4. Prove the detectors: press TREADLE TEST once. The CARROW LN. CALLS counter advances one figure, the Carrow Lane treadle marks light, and Carrow Lane receives its green at the next change of stage.
  5. To re-time, take hold of the wanted cam knob — MAIN GREEN CAM, SIDE GREEN CAM or WALK CAM — pull it towards you against the clutch, turn the drum to the new mark and let it spring back. The CYCLE TIMER sectors redraw to the new timings at once. Log the new settings in the site register.
  6. Leave the INTERVAL CRANK stowed unless the selector is at HAND; at HAND, wind the crank to drive the intervals, or use the POLICE PUSHBUTTON to step them.
  7. Use the EMERGENCY FLASH KEY only on the word of police or fire. To return, turn the key back and confirm the selector is at AUTO.
  8. Leave BELL CUT out (bell armed) when leaving the cabinet. Silence it only while an alarm is being attended.
  9. Close the cabinet. Working plant shows: POWER jewel lit, ON TRANSFER dark, voltmeter steady, flywheel turning, and no alarm standing.

Alarms

Indication Meaning Action
CONFLICT
(ON TRANSFER lit, junction flashing)		 The conflict monitor has seen a commanded aspect go dark: a filament has failed while its street held right of way. Attend. Clear the cause per Faults, press LAMP RESTORE, return the junction to service with the MODE SELECTOR.
CYCLE DRIFT A cam clutch is slipping: the cycle runs longer than the dials promise. Work any cam knob — pull, turn, release — to re-seat the clutch. Check the CYCLE TIMER against the settings before leaving.
SATURATION Queues have stood past thirteen vehicles a approach. At twenty seconds the bell gives warning; at ninety the controller flashes. Extend the greens with the cam knobs or clear the obstruction, then take the MODE SELECTOR to FLASH and back to AUTO to reset the trip.
DEMAND STUCK A Carrow Lane treadle has stood closed above half a minute; Fenn Road is held to minimum green meanwhile. Press TREADLE TEST once to free the contact. If the alarm stands, put the TREADLE STICK test down and report the detector.
MAINS LOW
(voltmeter under 216)		 The supply is sagging; the motor slows and every interval stretches. Examine the supply fuses and the isolator connections. The controller keeps time proportionally and recovers itself when the volts return.
Striker bell ringing An alarm stands and BELL CUT is armed. Answer it. Cut the bell only while you attend; always arm it again when you leave.

Faults and recovery

1. Signal lamp failure

What you see: a Carrow Lane red lens stands dark while Fenn Road holds its green; within a second the conflict monitor drops the junction to alternating red flash, lights ON TRANSFER, and raises the CONFLICT alarm with the bell.

Recovery:

  1. Open the MAINTENANCE TESTS guard and return the LAMP FAIL test switch to its down position (a genuine failure: isolate with the MAINS ISOLATOR and change the lamp).
  2. Press LAMP RESTORE. The monitor re-arms and the forced flash is released.
  3. If the selector has been moved, return it to AUTO and watch one full cycle.

2. Cam clutch slip

What you see: the cycle grows longer than the CYCLE TIMER sectors promise, the slip factor climbs, and the CYCLE DRIFT alarm stands. Queues build behind the stretched intervals and, if left, the SATURATION timer will flash the junction.

Recovery:

  1. Return the CLUTCH SLIP test switch to its down position.
  2. Re-seat the clutch: pull any cam knob towards you, turn it a little, and release it home. This alone clears the fault and the CYCLE DRIFT alarm.
  3. Check the cycle against the dial sectors. If the saturation timer has flashed the junction, take the MODE SELECTOR to FLASH and back to AUTO to reset the trip.

3. Treadle stuck closed

What you see: the Carrow Lane treadle marks never extinguish and the calls counter races. After twenty-five seconds the DEMAND STUCK alarm stands and the controller cuts Fenn Road to minimum green every cycle; the main street queue builds and can end in a SATURATION flash.

Recovery:

  1. Press TREADLE TEST once. The tap frees the stuck contact; the alarm clears at the next change of stage.
  2. If the marks still stand, open the MAINTENANCE TESTS guard, return the TREADLE STICK switch to its down position, and report the detector for attention.
  3. If the saturation timer has tripped the junction, restore with the MODE SELECTOR: to FLASH and back to AUTO.

Specifications

Electrical
Mains supply 240 V, 50 c/s, single phase
Motor synchronous, geared 1 rev. of camshaft per cycle
Signal load 4 heads × 3 aspects, 60 W lamps, plus 2 pedestrian units
Low-voltage notice alarm below 216 V; motor stalls below 178 V
Timing
Main green (MAIN GREEN CAM) 15–60 s in 5 s steps
Side green (SIDE GREEN CAM) 10–40 s in 5 s steps
Walk (WALK CAM) 5–15 s, never beyond side green less 3 s
Amber / clearance 3 s amber each street; 2 s all-red each change
Cycle, extremes of setting 35–110 s
Emergency flash alternating red, 0.7 s each way
Protection
Conflict monitor operates 0.8 s after a commanded aspect goes dark
Saturation timer warns at 20 s, trips to flash at 90 s beyond a 13-vehicle queue
Detector supervision DEMAND STUCK after 25 s of continuous call
Construction
Cabinet stove-enamelled steel, type M3/240, grey-green
Controller, complete c. 210 lb; floor-mounted; I.E.E. Regulations 1954 apply
