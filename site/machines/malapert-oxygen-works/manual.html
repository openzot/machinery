Overview

Train No. 1 reduces ilmenite-rich mare regolith with hot hydrogen and takes its oxygen back as water. Screened fines from the hopper fall through the counterflow preheat loop into fluidised-bed reductor R-1. At working temperature the hydrogen strips lattice oxygen; the offgas — water vapour in hydrogen — passes the cyclone, one of two twin dryer columns, and the electrolyser E-2, which returns dry hydrogen to the windbox and sends oxygen to the site header. Spent solids collect as a bath above the taphole and are cast as bricks through the crucible car.

The train is rated 70 kg/h of oxygen continuous at NOMINAL feed, 100 kg/h short-period. The bay stands on the ridge the crews call the Peak of Light: the sun does not move, but the array budget does not grow either. The whole console lives inside 640 kW. Everything you do here is a trade between bed heat, gas flow, dryer life and kilowatts.

The panel was built by the Consortium’s terrestrial licence partner to mine-winder practice: etched ceramic legends, mechanical counters, and monitoring circuits that stay alive on their own batteries when the bus is down. The annunciator is therefore truthful even with the breaker open.

Controls

SITE BREAKER
Sixty-millimetre rotary isolator, bottom left of SITE POWER. OFF / ON. Closing it energises the heater bus and charges the recycle loop with hydrogen (the purity cell reads after about three seconds). Opening it drops banks, feed and blower; the bed cools on its own losses.
HEATER BANK
Three toggle switches A, B and C under a hinged guard — lift the cover before the toggles will move. A = 120 kW, B = 140 kW, C = 160 kW. Bank C is the trim bank; the site load relay will shed it automatically twenty seconds into an overload, and hold it out until you acknowledge and re-close.
FEED RATE
Five-position rotary selector: CLOSED · TRICKLE · LOW · NOMINAL · FULL, that is 0 / 6 / 14 / 22 / 30 kg/min of screened fines. Each click advances one position. The hopper top-up conveyor runs itself; watch its percentage on the mimic.
𝕊₂ BLOWER
Notched quadrant lever, five positions 0–4, driving the recycle circulator. Notch 2 is the working notch for TRICKLE and LOW; NOMINAL feed wants notch 3. Notch 4 over-blasts the bed — the ΔP transmitter will call it before the bed does.
TAPHOLE PLUG
The guarded station centre-right. Lift the hinged guard (this arms CRACK and frees the TAP lever), press and hold CRACK for about two seconds to break the refractory plug, then ease the notched TAP lever through SHUT · NIP · OPEN · FULL. Closing the lever lets the plug sinter over again in about six seconds while the bed is hot. Closing the guard snaps the lever shut.
DRYER SELECTOR
Two-position key switch, columns A and B. The keyed column dries the loop; the other regenerates. A saturated column needs about ninety-five seconds offline to come back fresh. Change columns before the dewpoint monitor complains, not after.
CYCLONE PURGE
Guarded pushbutton under GAS TRAIN. Hold it to fire plant air back up the cyclone screen: dust loading falls while held, and a blinded impulse line clears in fifteen to twenty seconds. It costs 70 kW and it only works with the breaker closed.
FAULT SELECTOR
Maintenance rotary under FAULT SIMULATION: OFF · CYC · DRY · TAP. Selects which simulated condition the injector applies. It affects the simulation only — no plant equipment is touched.
FAULT INJECT
Collared momentary button beside the selector. Presses the selected simulated fault into the train exactly as the real condition would present: same transmitters, same annunciator windows, same recoveries as Section “Faults and recovery”.

Every control works from the keyboard: TAB walks the panel, ENTER and SPACE operate buttons and guards, arrow keys walk the two lever quadrants.

Normal operation

  1. Walk the panel: confirm all seven annunciator windows dark, FEED RATE at CLOSED, BLOWER at 0, TAP lever SHUT and guarded.
  2. Close the SITE BREAKER. The purity cell wakes and reads about 96% once the loop charges.
  3. Lift the HEATER BANK guard and switch banks A and B. From cold the bed reaches 850 °C in roughly three minutes; the sight port goes from black glass through deep red.
  4. At or above 850 °C, run the 𝕊₂ BLOWER to notch 2. Watch BED ΔP settle between 11 and 14 kPa — that is the bed standing up.
  5. When BED °C passes 980, put FEED RATE to TRICKLE. Oxygen flow should show within seconds and purity should hold near 96%.
  6. Trim toward the money band, 1040–1095 °C. TRICKLE sits there on A+B alone; for NOMINAL feed switch bank C in as well (562 kW all-in — inside the budget) and take the blower to notch 3. If BED TEMP climbs past 1120 you are over-banked: drop feed first, banks second.
  7. Watch BATH on the mimic — spent solids gather whether or not you are casting. Above about 40% prepare to tap: lift the TAPHOLE PLUG guard and hold CRACK until the state cell reads PLUG CRACKED.
  8. Ease TAP to NIP, then OPEN. At OPEN with a hot bed the crucible fills in about two and a half minutes and drains the bath faster than NOMINAL feed refills it. FULL pours harder than any continuous feed can fill — use it to pull a high bath down.
  9. At CAST 100% return the lever to SHUT at once. The car swaps in five seconds, the brick count climbs by thirteen, and the hot plug sinters itself over.
  10. Steady state is NOMINAL feed, three banks, blower notch 3, a tap every couple of minutes, and the load bar comfortably short of 640 kW. To shut down: FEED CLOSED, banks off in reverse order, give the bath a last full tap, then open the breaker when the sight port dims.

Alarms

The annunciator is battery-backed and truthful with the breaker open. Flashing means new and unacknowledged; ACK steadies whatever is showing. A window that comes back flashing is a new event. When REACTOR TRIP shows, ACK also resets the trip interlock after you have dealt with the cause.

Window Means Do
SITE LOAD HIGH Console demand exceeds the 640 kW array budget. Shed load: drop the blower a notch or bank C. After 20 s the relay sheds C itself; after 45 s the train trips.
BED ΔP HIGH Bed head above 16.5 kPa — over-blast, a loaded cyclone screen, or a false head from a blinded impulse line. If the blower is at 4, come back a notch. Otherwise purge (below) and watch the dust note on the mimic.
BED TEMP HIGH Bed above 1120 °C. Cut feed, then banks. Twelve seconds over 1160 °C trips the train on BED OVERTEMPERATURE.
𝕊₂ PURITY LOW Dewpoint monitor sees moisture below 88% — a saturated duty column, or the loop drifting with the circulator off. Change DRYER SELECTOR to the fresh column. Purity must climb back through 92% before the window clears.
BATH HIGH Bath above 88% of capacity, or slag spilling from an overfilled crucible. Tap now; if the crucible is full, SHUT first, let the car swap, then crack and pour. Bath held above 98% trips the train in fifteen seconds.
TAP STALL The taphole plug has seized — prove circuit refuses, pour stops if you were pouring. Re-drill per Faults and recovery. Twenty-five seconds of lever held open against a seized hole trips the train.
REACTOR TRIP Protective trip: overtemperature, fluidisation lost, electrolyser flood, overload, full bath or blocked taphole. Banks, feed and blower are already dropped. Read the reason on the mimic mode cell, deal with the cause, then ACK to reset the interlock and restart per Normal operation.

Faults and recovery

cyclone blinding

You see: BED ΔP HIGH without touching anything; the CYCLONE note walks CLEAN → LOADED → BLINDED; running, the true bed head climbs until the bed channels and oxygen yield sags.

  1. Note the load bar. The purge draws 70 kW — shed a bank or a blower notch first if you are near the budget.
  2. Lift the PURGE guard and hold CYCLONE PURGE.
  3. Hold until the note reads CLEAN and BED ΔP returns to your notch’s normal figure — typically fifteen to twenty seconds.
  4. Release. If the window re-lights within the shift, suspect the feed screen and drop to TRICKLE while you investigate.

dryer breakthrough

You see: the dewpoint monitor calls 𝕊₂ PURITY LOW within seconds regardless of flow; the duty column shows saturated; flowing, purity decays toward the mid-seventies and oxygen yield falls away.

  1. Turn DRYER SELECTOR to the other column. This is the whole recovery — do it early.
  2. Purity recovers through 92% in a few minutes of flow and the window clears.
  3. The wet column regenerates offline in about ninety-five seconds and may be selected again once it reads fresh.
  4. Ignored below 75% purity, the electrolysers flood and the train trips in forty seconds.

tap-hole freeze

You see: the state cell reads PLUG SEIZED, TAP STALL lights after eight seconds, and an open pour stops dead. CRACK will not take while the seizure holds.

  1. Return TAP to SHUT. Lever held open against a seized hole trips the train after twenty-five seconds.
  2. Bring the bed above 850 °C — banks A+B will hold it — a cold hole re-seizes even after drilling.
  3. Lift the taphole guard and hold CRACK for three seconds. The state cell returns to PLUG INTACT, then cracks normally.
  4. Resume the tap promptly. If the alarm was raised while casting, expect the first crucible to read light.
