Wrayburn Instrument Works · Sheffield · Instruction Book No. 47b

High-Vacuum Evaporating Bench Mk. III

Instructions for the care and operation of Pattern WB-1119, as installed in the University laboratory. Issued to the Operator with the bench; a second copy is kept in the bench book.

Overview

The bench deposits thin metal films upon glass by evaporation in high vacuum. Inside the bell jar hangs a carriage of glasswork above an electrically heated filament charged with slugs of metal. When the air is withdrawn - first by the rotary oil pump, then by the boiling-oil diffusion pump beneath the jar - the filament is run up to a dull white heat. Metal leaves it in straight lines, at the speed of a gas molecule, and buries whatever the shutter is pleased to uncover.

The work is wanted for lens contracts: a quarter-wave of fluorite or a film of metal, laid evenly enough that a whole crate of objectives behaves alike. The vacuum is the whole art. A film laid at a poor vacuum is cloudy, and a filament flashed in air is gone in four seconds - the Ministry does not extend contracts for either reason.

Three gauges tell the story. The chamber dial reads the jar over nine decades; the small foreline dial watches the pipe between diffusion pump and rotary pump, where a diffusion pump drowns if neglected; and the McLeod gauge - a column of mercury in glass which the Operator tilts by hand - is the court of appeal when a meter is doubted.

Controls

Control Type Action
MAINS SWITCH guarded toggle Energises the panel, the pump motor and the heaters. The sheet-metal guard must be swung open (press GUARD) before the toggle can be moved; closing the guard trips the switch.
COOLING WATER COCK quarter-turn lever Admits cooling water to the diffusion-pump jacket and condenser. Watch the sight glass: bubbles should ride steadily while the cock is open. Water flows whenever the cock is open, mains or no mains.
PUMP SELECTOR rotary switch, three positions OFF leaves everything sealed. ROUGHING connects the rotary pump to the bell jar and to the foreline together. BACKING disconnects the jar and lets the rotary pump hold the foreline only, freeing the diffusion pump to work against it.
BAFFLE VALVE slow-throw lever Opens the line from jar to diffusion pump. Geared, and slow on purpose: allow the full travel before expecting the chamber gauge to answer.
AIR ADMIT VALVE small cock Lets dry air into the jar for taking off lids and changing work. Open it only when the heater is cold or standing low.
DIFFUSION PUMP HEATER rotary knob, 0-450 W Fires the oil boiler. Below some 150 W the pump only dreams; working heat is 350-450 W. Standby heat (150 W) may be held overnight.
FILAMENT CURRENT rotary knob, 0-30 A Heats the evaporation filament. Some 6 A degasses the charge; 22-26 A evaporates it. Above 10-3 mm Hg a hot filament burns; see Faults and recovery.
SHUTTER LEVER slide lever Passes or intercepts the metal stream between filament and glasswork. Open it only for timed deposition; close it to end the batch.
MCLEOD TILT LEVER hold-to-read lever Tilts the McLeod gauge so the mercury climbs and traps a charge of gas in the closed limb; hold it while the reading is taken from the drum window, release and the mercury falls home. The gauge reads nothing until it is tilted.
ALARM ACCEPT pushbutton Dims the illuminated annunciator flags once they have been seen. It silences nothing and cures nothing; an alarm still lit after acceptance is still true.
INDICATOR LAMPS TEST pushbutton Lights every flag and jewel at once. Test at the start of each shift.
FAULT TEST SWITCHES three toggles under the tray flap Instruction rig: puts a JAR CRACK, VALVE PASSING or FILAMENT BURN condition on the bench without harming it. Switch off, or clear by the repairs, after the drill.
REPAIR ACTIONS four pushbuttons Enter the steward's remedy: JAR REPLACED after a new jar is bedded, VALVE RESEATED after grinding the foreline valve, FILAMENT REPLACED after fitting a fresh filament, PUMP OIL CHANGED after the diffusion oil has been run out and refilled.
MASTER RESET mushroom push Returns the bench to its cold state: heaters off, filament fresh, faults cleared, jar stored under rough vacuum. Used between teaching sessions; never as a shortcut during a batch.

Normal operation

  1. Bench book open. Last entry read. INDICATOR LAMPS TEST pressed; every flag and jewel answered.
  2. Open the guard, put the MAINS SWITCH ON. Pump-motor pulley turns freely.
  3. Open the COOLING WATER COCK and confirm flow in the sight glass.
  4. Put the PUMP SELECTOR to ROUGHING. The jar falls from its stored rough vacuum toward two thousandths of a millimetre over some minutes. Do not hurry it.
  5. When the foreline dial stands below one tenth, move the PUMP SELECTOR to BACKING. The rotary pump now serves the foreline alone.
  6. Throw the BAFFLE VALVE fully open; wait out its travel.
  7. Raise the DIFFUSION PUMP HEATER to 400-450 W. The viewer shows cherry red, the jet cone stands, and the chamber dial runs down through the decades toward 10-5 mm Hg. This fall takes minutes; talk to it and it goes slower.
  8. Prove the vacuum: hold the MCLEOD TILT LEVER down, read the drum, compare with the dial. If they disagree by more than a factor of two, believe the McLeod and stop.
  9. Charge set? Run the FILAMENT CURRENT to 6 A for fifteen seconds to drive off damp; the chamber will bump - let it settle again below 10-4.
  10. Raise FILAMENT CURRENT to the working figure (22-26 A) with the SHUTTER LEVER still closed. Confirm the DEPOSITING jewel stays dark and the chamber holds.
  11. Open the SHUTTER LEVER and hold time by the watch; the optical monitor and thickness drums climb. Close the SHUTTER LEVER at the figure wanted.
  12. Run FILAMENT CURRENT back to zero. Drop the DIFFUSION PUMP HEATER to zero (or to standby), keep water running until the viewer is black, close the BAFFLE VALVE, and only then admit air by the AIR ADMIT VALVE to change work.
  13. Enter jar number, filament current, thickness and time in the bench book.

Alarms

Flag Meaning To do
VACUUM LEAK The jar is losing ground faster than outgassing explains - a cracked jar, a lifted lid-seal. If the AIR ADMIT VALVE was opened on purpose the flag is kept dark. Stop deposition at once. Isolate with the BAFFLE VALVE if the leak is violent; find and cure, then re-rough. A confirmed jar goes to the REPAIR ACTIONS press marked JAR REPLACED once the new jar is bedded.
BACKING PRESSURE HIGH The foreline has run past about a fifth of a millimetre. The diffusion pump is near drowning and will spit oil into the jar if worked so. Restore pumping: selector to BACKING or roughing as the case deserves. If a film was being laid, condemn it - the monitor will show haze. Persistent rise means the foreline valve passes; grind and use VALVE RESEATED.
COOLING WATER LOSS The heater is firing but the jacket is dry - cock shut, or the supply failed. Open the COOLING WATER COCK. If supply is lost outright, take the DIFFUSION PUMP HEATER to zero and wait the pump cold before restarting water.
PUMP OVERHEAT Body past 88°C: the trip has cut the heater, and it stays cut until the pump cools near 46°C. Water on; heater to zero; accept the flag and wait. Repeated overheating means the coil is furred - report it.
FILAMENT O/C The filament is burnt open - flashed in a poor vacuum, or worn out at last. Nothing will bring it back. Current to zero, fit a new filament and slug, press FILAMENT REPLACED, re-evacuate, and begin the batch again.
An accepted flag is not an absence of alarm. ALARM ACCEPT dims the lamp; the condition remains until it is cured, and the lamp relights bright on any new event.

Faults and recovery

Bell jar crack

You see: the VACUUM LEAK flag; the chamber dial climbing although the pumps run true; hiss at the seal if you listen.
Recovery: close the SHUTTER LEVER, run FILAMENT CURRENT to zero, and swing the BAFFLE VALVE closed so a violent leak cannot drown the diffusion pump. Take the jar off, bed a sound jar on its gasket, press JAR REPLACED among the REPAIR ACTIONS, and re-rough from step 4 of Normal operation. The MASTER RESET also clears the fault, but wastes the session's heat.

Foreline valve passing

You see: BACKING PRESSURE HIGH with everything shut; the foreline dial creeping up hour by hour even at cold.
Recovery: run the PUMP SELECTOR to BACKING and confirm the dial comes back; grind the valve at the next opportunity, then press VALVE RESEATED. Until ground, expect the flag again within the half-hour of any standstill.

Filament burnout

You see: FILAMENT O/C immediately; the wire in the jar gone at its brightest point; if it burned mid-batch, the thickness drums stop and the film is likely hazy besides.
Recovery: FILAMENT CURRENT to zero. Fit the new filament and charge, press FILAMENT REPLACED, and return to Normal operation step 7. Remember why it went: above 10-3 mm Hg a working filament oxidises away in seconds - the vacuum is proved before the current is raised, never after.

Oil contamination (cloudy films, a pump that will not reach 10-5) follows from ignoring BACKING PRESSURE HIGH while the boiler is hot. Run the oil out, refill clean, and enter it with PUMP OIL CHANGED.
