Overview

This console runs the atmosphere of the Dunlin Sentinel's saturation complex: the living chamber, the transfer lock beneath it, and the gas that keeps six men alive at storage depth. The chamber sits at depth for weeks at a time. The men do not come up to you; everything they breathe is your doing, from this rack, on this deck, under this fluorescent light.

The console does four things and nothing else. It holds chamber depth against a setpoint by admitting heliox from one of the four quad banks. It holds oxygen partial pressure between 16 and 25 per cent by a needle-valve injection from the cylinder pair. It holds carbon dioxide down by washing the chamber atmosphere through scrubber canisters on two electric fans. It tells you, loudly, when any of those three things is slipping.

BREATHING GAS. Everything aft of the isolator is at up to 230 bar, and everything forward of it is at storage depth pressure. Heliox under pressure strips the air out of a space faster than a man can walk through the door. Work the vent before you work anything else, and never trust a gauge until the LAMPS TEST has proved the panel.

Controls

Control Type Positions / range Action
MAINS ISOLATOR key switch OFF · ON Energises the console. OFF drops indication only; the chamber vent is mechanical and works regardless.
LAMPS TEST momentary push press Lights every annunciator window. Prove the panel before every shift.
ALARM ACCEPT push press Steadies flashing windows so new events still catch the eye. Does not clear any condition.
PANEL RESET push press Returns the console to its cold state: chamber at surface, setpoint zero, fans off, alarms clear, faults cleared.
SCRUBBER FANS rotary selector OFF · LOW · HIGH · STBY CO2 scrubber duty. HIGH is the running position. STBY starts the standby fan train when the duty train has failed.
QUAD SELECTOR guarded rotary A · B · C · D Selects which quad bank feeds make-up. Change over with the guard raised, watching the bank needles.
O2 INJECT micrometer needle valve 0 – 8 turns Trims oxygen partial pressure upward. A quarter turn moves ppO2 about six points an hour; watch the bargraph minute to minute. Count the turns on the drum.
MAKE-UP ISOLATOR guarded toggle SHUT · AUTO LINES UP or ISOLATES the automatic make-up valve. SHUT is also the stop for a passing valve.
MAKE-UP BYPASS mushroom, hold-down hold Admits helium manually while held, bypassing the auto valve. Use after isolating a passing valve, or to top the last metres.
CHAMBER VENT vernier knob 0 – 10 turns Vents gas to sea to shallow the chamber. Below ten metres the outflow carries the atmosphere with it: full vent flushes carbon dioxide fast and strips oxygen with it — watch the bargraph fall as the CO2 does. Mechanical: it works with the panel dead.
DEPTH SET pot dial 0 – 120 mSW Storage-depth setpoint. The yellow ghost needle on the big gauge shows where you have asked her to sit.
TRIP RESET push press Rearms the life-support trip after the cause has been cleared. Refuses while CO2 or oxygen is still out of band.
FAULT TESTS three toggles under cover FAN · BANK · VALVE Maintenance injection of each fault for drill and proof of the alarm chain. Return all to centre-down after use.

Normal operation

From cold (PANEL RESET state, chamber at surface) to sitting on storage depth with a clean board:

  1. Confirm the board cold: no window lit, TRIP RESET reading CLEAR, both verniers closed, DEPTH SET at zero.
  2. Press LAMPS TEST. Every window lights amber or red; release and prove all went out.
  3. Turn MAINS ISOLATOR ON if it is not already. The SUPPLY LIVE neon burns; the needles wake.
  4. Put SCRUBBER FANS to HIGH. The blower hum comes up through the frame.
  5. Raise the QUAD SELECTOR guard and read the four bank needles. Feed from the fullest bank; leave the thinnest bank for the bell run. Select it.
  6. Confirm MAKE-UP ISOLATOR is lined up AUTO.
  7. Set DEPTH SET to storage depth — one hundred metres for this charter. The yellow ghost needle swings; the black needle walks the chamber down at not more than a third of a metre a second. Expect the chamber temperature to rise a degree or two on the way down.
  8. As the depth passes forty metres, open O2 INJECT about half a turn and trim to hold ppO2 at twenty per cent on the bargraph. Small turns. Watch it for five minutes before you touch it again.
  9. When the black needle sits on the ghost, ease SCRUBBER FANS to LOW for quiet hours if CO2 stays under ten millibars.
  10. Accept any standing alarm with ALARM ACCEPT once you have understood it, and log bank pressures hourly. The banks tell you the story of the dive before anything else does.

Alarms

Window Lens Means Do
PP O2 HIGH red Oxygen above 25 % — injection overshooting or cylinders leaking into the line. Close O2 INJECT. Vent if above 28 %. Above 31 % sustained the trip fires itself.
PP O2 LOW red Oxygen below 16 % — consumption outrunning injection. Open O2 INJECT a quarter turn and watch the bargraph climb. Below 13 % sustained the men go to sleep before you can blink.
CO2 HIGH red Carbon dioxide above 30 mbar surface equivalent. SCRUBBER FANS to HIGH. If it will not turn over, see FAN FAIL. At 50 mbar for a minute the life-support trip drops everything.
DEPTH DEV. amber Chamber more than three metres off setpoint. Check SUPPLY LOW first, then the isolator lineup. Hold MAKE-UP BYPASS or ease CHAMBER VENT to bring her back by hand.
SUPPLY LOW amber Selected quad bank below 40 bar. Change QUAD SELECTOR to a full bank. Never let the feed die with men at depth.
FAN FAIL amber Duty scrubber train tripped on overload. SCRUBBER FANS to STBY. The standby train carries the chamber until the electricians earn their keep.
O2 CYL LOW amber Cylinder pair below 50 bar. Line up the reserve rack per the gas officer's plan; this console takes either pair on its own manifold.
MK-UP PASSING amber Flow seen past the automatic valve with no demand. Flip MAKE-UP ISOLATOR to SHUT. Recover depth on MAKE-UP BYPASS and CHAMBER VENT until the fitters change the seat.

Faults and recovery

Scrubber fan stall

You see: FAN FAIL within seconds; then CO2 HIGH climbing as the wash dies, and the chamber TV fogging as the temperature stratifies. Ignored, at fifty millibars the life-support trip drops the duty fans and locks compression — the TRIP RESET reads TRIPPED · CO2. Only the standby position still turns, on its own feeders.

Recovery: put SCRUBBER FANS to STBY; the standby train picks up the wash and CO2 falls. When the board is clean below thirty millibars, press TRIP RESET — it refuses while the cause stands, and it is meant to.

Quad bank depletion

You see: the selected bank needle falling far faster than the makeup rate explains, SUPPLY LOW at forty bar, then DEPTH DEV. as the feed starves and the chamber drifts shallow.

Recovery: raise the QUAD SELECTOR guard and select a live bank. The drain stops with the selection; the fault lamp on FAULT TESTS clears. Re-establish depth against the ghost needle and log the flat bank OUT OF SERVICE on the slate.

Make-up valve passing

You see: MK-UP PASSING immediately — flow where there should be none — then DEPTH DEV. as she creeps past setpoint toward the stops, and O2 CYL LOW later if the dilution drags the oxygen down with it.

Recovery: flip the MAKE-UP ISOLATOR guard down to SHUT; the passing stops with the line. Bring the depth back by hand: hold MAKE-UP BYPASS to add metres, ease CHAMBER VENT to lose them, until the black needle kisses the ghost. Leave the isolator SHUT and run manual until the fitters have the seat changed.
