MV TANGLEWING — CUT & WINCH CONSOLE

Operating Manual M-2084 · firmware 84.7.2 · for the kelp harvester outfit CW-0331 as fitted aboard the vessel.

APPROVED · CREW USE

Overview

The console drives the Tanglewing's subsea harvest gear: a cutter sled that rides a winch cable through the farmed Laminaria canopy while the vessel crawls the bank. One operator runs the whole chain from this desk — ship bus, hydraulic circuit, winch depth, cutter engagement, haul speed and the debris wash — against the amber echosounder picture of the bank.

The sled works best inside the canopy band drawn on the sounder. Cut too shallow and you take water and weed-tips; touch the seabed and you take sand, stones and torque. Everything the sled does comes back to the desk as cable tension, cutter torque and oil temperature. Keep the first two out of their red arcs and the third below 92 °C, and the gear will fill the hold all shift.

Controls

Control Type Action and positions
SYSTEM BUS Guarded breaker toggle Powers the console and both hydraulic circuits. OFF / ON. Dropping it stops the haul and the cutter at once.
HYD CIRCUIT Three-position rotary SHUT — circuits depressurised; MAIN — full 205 bar working circuit; STBY — reduced 168 bar standby circuit. Passing through SHUT re-seats the main quick-release coupling.
TRIM SELECTOR Three-position rotary BOW / LEVEL / STERN pump transfer. LEVEL is the harvesting attitude; use BOW or STERN in short nudges only.
DEPTH WHEEL Spoked handwheel Commands the winch depth, 4–18 m. The wheel spins as you pay; the drum below the sounder turns as the wire actually pays out. Arrow keys work in 0.2 m steps, with Shift for 1 m.
CUTTER ENGAGE Guarded toggle under a perspex flap LIFT the flap, then switch ON to spin the cutter head. The switch is dead while LOCKOUT is lit.
HARVEST LEVER Four-notch lever quadrant STOP / SLOW / HALF / FULL haul along the bank. The notch engages only with bus on, pressure up and the cable intact.
DEBRIS WASH Three-way rocker group OFF / WASH — continuous seawater flush over the blade; PURGE — five-second reverse blast that clears fouling, then drops back to OFF by itself.
DRUM BRAKE Hold-on push bar Hold to lock the winch drum. Held while the brake is bleeding it re-seats the brake lining (five seconds).
HOLD TRANSFER Momentary pushbutton Hold to pump the hold ashore or to the mother lighter, about one tonne a second with pressure up.
ALARM ACK Pushbutton Steadies flashing alarm tiles and silences the horn. Held two seconds with the gear lost overboard it re-rigs a replacement cable (see Alarms).
LAMP TEST Pushbutton, hold Lights every jewel lamp and alarm tile while held.
FAULT TESTS Hinged maintenance cover, three buttons Crew-only injection of the three test faults: FOUL, SLIP, LEAK. Used for drills; each behaves exactly like the real failure.
SOUND Rocker Enables the pump hum and the alarm horn. Sound never plays until you first touch the console.

Normal operation

  1. Power up. Raise SYSTEM BUS to ON. The BUS jewel burns green and the status window reads NO HYD PRESSURE.
  2. Pressurise. Turn HYD CIRCUIT to MAIN. The gauge climbs to about 205 bar and the HYD PR jewel goes green. STBY gives about 168 bar and half cutting appetite — use it only to limp home.
  3. Trim. Set TRIM SELECTOR to LEVEL and let the boat settle.
  4. Open the wash. Set DEBRIS WASH to WASH. The WASH jewel lights.
  5. Command depth. Wheel DEPTH WHEEL until CMD reads mid-canopy — normally 10–12 m; read the band top/bottom figure under the sounder. The orange bug on the ruler shows your command.
  6. Engage the cutter. Lift the perspex guard and switch CUTTER ENGAGE ON. Torque settles near ten per cent spinning free. The drum pays out and ACTUAL walks down to the bug.
  7. Haul away. Take HARVEST LEVER to SLOW. When the sled is riding inside the dashed canopy band and TORQUE is steady below sixty, step up to HALF — FULL only on thin, clean weed.
  8. Watch the arc. Keep ACTUAL inside the band, TENSION below 175 kN, OIL below 92 °C and TORQUE out of the red. Wheel gently: every metre of command error adds thirty kilonewtons.
  9. Empty the hold. At HOLD FULL, hold HOLD TRANSFER against the lighter until the hold glass reads under 7 t, then resume. The cutter starves above eight tonnes.
  10. Stopping. HARVEST LEVER to STOP; CUTTER ENGAGE OFF; wheel the sled clear of the canopy; DEBRIS WASH to OFF; HYD CIRCUIT to SHUT; SYSTEM BUS OFF.
CAUTION Never wheel deeper than the seabed trace. Grounding the sled spikes torque, heats the oil toward the trip point and blunts a crown of blades you cannot change at sea.

Alarms

Tiles flash until ALARM ACK steadies them; they go out when the condition clears. The horn repeats until acknowledged.

Tile Meaning What to do
CUTTER FOUL Rope, net or rag on the cutter head; torque high, yield falling. Purge it — see Faults and recovery.
BRAKE SLIP Winch brake bleeding; sled runs deeper than commanded, tension climbing. Re-seat the brake — see Faults and recovery.
HYD LEAK Weep at the main coupling; reservoir falling on the MAIN circuit. Isolate the weep — see Faults and recovery.
TENSION Cable load above 175 kN, or the cable already parted. Ease haul, zero the depth error. If the cable has parted, follow RE-RIG below.
OIL HOT Oil past 92 °C and climbing to the 96 °C trip. Stop the cut, ease haul to zero, keep WASH on, let it cool under 78 °C, then ACK to arm restart.
OFF CANOPY Sled more than half a metre outside the band, or near the seabed. Wheel back into the band. Advisory only, but it is costing you tonnage or blades.
HOLD FULL Eight tonnes aboard. Hold HOLD TRANSFER until under seven tonnes.
LOCKOUT A trip is holding the gear dead: thermal trip, or parted cable. Thermal: cool and ACK. Parted: RE-RIG below.

LOCKOUT release

  1. Thermal trip: lever STOP, engage OFF; keep DEBRIS WASH on WASH. When OIL reads under 78 °C press ALARM ACK once — LOCKOUT goes out and the engage will accept again.
  2. Parted cable: the gear is gone overboard. Put SYSTEM BUS OFF, press and hold ALARM ACK for two seconds — the console rigs the spare cable, and LOCKOUT and TENSION go out. Return the bus to ON and start again from step 4 of Normal operation.

Faults and recovery

cutter fouling

You see: CUTTER FOUL flashing; TORQUE jumps sixty points and stays there; the hold barely fills though the lever is on notch; a slow pull on TENSION.

Recovery:

  1. HARVEST LEVER to STOP.
  2. Set DEBRIS WASH to PURGE. The reverse blast runs five seconds by itself, then drops to OFF.
  3. Torque falls as the foul tears away. Below about thirty per cent, re-engage CUTTER ENGAGE if it dropped out, and haul away again.
  4. If you ignore it the trapped load grinds the oil hot and the console will trip on temperature within a couple of minutes.

winch brake slip

You see: BRAKE SLIP flashing; ACTUAL drifts deeper than CMD whatever the wheel does; ERR grows negative; TENSION climbs toward the red arc. Past 214 kN for a breath the cable parts — TENSION and LOCKOUT latch, the sled is lost.

Recovery, while the wire is still on the drum:

  1. HARVEST LEVER to STOP.
  2. Wheel DOWN to bring CMD onto the running depth so ERR reads near zero and TENSION falls under 175 kN.
  3. Press and hold DRUM BRAKE a full five seconds. The lining re-seats; BRAKE SLIP steadies and goes out.
  4. Wheel back to the working depth and resume. If the cable parted instead, follow the RE-RIG procedure under Alarms.

hydraulic fluid leak

You see: HYD LEAK flashing from the first weep; the OIL LVL glass falls about one per cent a second on the MAIN circuit; under about forty-five per cent the gauge collapses and the cutter fades to a stop.

Recovery:

  1. HARVEST LEVER to STOP and CUTTER ENGAGE OFF.
  2. Turn HYD CIRCUIT to SHUT. Bleeding down re-seats the main coupling and the leak lamp goes out.
  3. For the rest of the shift work on STBY: 168 bar, gentler appetite, and the level will make itself up from the reserve tank.
  4. Main circuit may be resumed once the level passes ninety.

Specifications

Winch gear
Cable 28 mm galvanised, 240 kN proof, parting detected at 214 kN sustained 2.5 s
Pay rate 0.55 m/s at full pressure
Depth range 4–18 m commanded; seabed limit enforced by software at 0.15 m above bottom
Cutting sled
Cutter bar 3.1 m twin reciprocating, hydraulic
Nominal torque 40–95 % in canopy; alarm at 120, gauge red beyond 125 %
Yield up to 13.5 t/h at HALF in medium weed
Hydraulics
Working pressure MAIN 205 bar · STBY 168 bar · SHUT 0
Reservoir 90 L ISO-HM 68; makeup from reserve at 0.22 %/s when isolated
Temperature Alarm 92 °C, trip 96 °C, restart permitted under 78 °C
Vessel & hold
Advance speeds SLOW 0.35 · HALF 0.72 · FULL 1.15 m/s at full pressure
Hold 8.0 t; transfer rate ≈0.95 t/s with pressure up
Ship supply 400 V, 50 Hz, console dry weight 214 kg
