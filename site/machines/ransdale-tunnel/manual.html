Overview

Tunnel No. 1 is an intermittent (blowdown) supersonic wind tunnel. A two-stage compressor charges a bank of two steel bottles with dry air; a quick-opening run valve releases this charge through an interchangeable nozzle block to give steady flow at Mach 1.6, 2.0, 2.5 or 3.0 past a wing model carried on an articulated sting. A pair of Schlieren mirrors makes the density gradients visible at the test-section window, so the bow shock and expansion fan are seen live while a twin wire balance reads lift and drag beneath the bench.

The air is used straight from storage: a full charge gives roughly fifty seconds at Mach 3 or a minute and a half at Mach 1.6, and then the tanks must be recharged. Two things ruin runs and neither forgives carelessness. Moisture condenses in the working section when the air is cold and wet, and it fouls the Schlieren optics for the rest of the shift. And beyond its critical incidence the model buffets; hold it there and the sting will fatigue, dump your charge through the exhaust and spend a fortnight in the fitters' shop. Keep the air dry, keep the shocks where they belong, and she will pay her way.

The desk is arranged along the air path, left to right across the top diagram: compressor, dryer, tank bank, settling chamber, test section, diffuser, exhaust. Start-up runs down the left-hand sequence rail; readings sit under your right hand.

Controls

Control Type and positions Action
MAIN BREAKER Rotary handle, OFF / ON. Isolates the desk supply. Powers the compressor, dryer heater, annunciator, lamps and Schlieren spark. The pneumatic run valve remains mechanical.
AIR DRYER HEATER Toggle, OFF / ON, amber pilot. Regenerates the silica-gel dryer and pre-heats charge air. Leave ON during recharge; the DRYER WET window must be dark before a run.
COMPRESSOR MOTOR Heavy toggle, STOP / START. Drives the two-stage compressor. Refuses to start while a thermal latch or winding fault stands.
MACH BLOCK SELECTOR Four-position rotary: 1.6 / 2.0 / 2.5 / 3.0. Selects the nozzle block in circuit. The blocks are bolted: the selector is dead while flow is established.
DIFFUSER BLEED Micrometer knob, SHUT to OPEN, percent window. Sets secondary bleed into the diffuser. Open it generously to start, trim back for running; too little bleed at high Mach swallows the shock (UNSTART).
RUN VALVE Snap lever, SHUT / OPEN with red open zone. Quick-opening gate between tank bank and nozzle. Throw it fully open in one motion - half-open seats buzz and heat. It locks after an emergency dump until TRIP RESET releases it.
ANGLE OF ATTACK HANDWHEEL Six-spoke handwheel, −5° to +15° incidence. Sweeps the model on its sting. Read incidence on the drum beneath the window. Dead after STING DMG until the trip is reset.
ALARM ACCEPT Pushbutton. Steadies flashing windows and silences the buzzer. Windows remain lit while their condition stands.
LAMPS TEST Pushbutton. Lights every jewel and annunciator window while held for two seconds.
TRIP RESET Red-capped pushbutton. Releases latched trips (motor overload, unstart lock, sting damage, fouled optics) once the underlying condition has been put right. It refuses, politely, if you have not.
COLD RESET Keyswitch, quarter turn. Returns the whole machine to cold, clear of alarms and trips.
FAULT TEST SWITCHES Three guarded toggles under a hinged cover. Injects MOISTURE INGRESS, STING OSCILLATION or MOTOR OVERLOAD for proving the alarm chain. Lift the cover before testing.
SOUND CUT Pushbutton, pressed = silent. Cuts the valve clack, airflow rush and buzzer loudspeaker.

For demonstration purposes the desk may also be worked entirely from a keyboard: every control takes Tab focus, rotary and knob controls answer to the arrow keys (Home and End travel to their stops, Shift speeds the handwheel), and pushbuttons answer Space or Enter.

Normal operation

  1. Turn MAIN BREAKER to ON. The POWER jewel lights and the annunciator lamps prove dark.
  2. Press LAMPS TEST; every window and jewel must light. Accept the test with ALARM ACCEPT.
  3. Switch AIR DRYER HEATER ON. If DRYER WET shows, wait for regeneration - the gel needs about a minute of heating.
  4. Start COMPRESSOR MOTOR and charge the tanks. Watch the motor temperature: above 90°C give the motor a rest. Charge to at least 210 bar until TANKS CHARGED burns.
  5. Stop the compressor before a run, and confirm DRYER WET is dark.
  6. With no flow, select the nozzle block on MACH BLOCK SELECTOR.
  7. Set DIFFUSER BLEED generous for starting: about 60% at Mach 2, nearer 80% at Mach 3.
  8. Bring ANGLE OF ATTACK HANDWHEEL to zero incidence.
  9. Throw the RUN VALVE fully OPEN in one motion. Settling pressure will stand near tank pressure less ten percent; the Mach drum counts up to its block value and RUNNING lights blue.
  10. Trim DIFFUSER BLEED down towards the blue band on the diffuser gauge while UNSTART stays dark. This lengthens the useful charge.
  11. Sweep the ANGLE OF ATTACK HANDWHEEL slowly, reading lift and drag and watching the shocks cross the window. Keep BUFFET dark; the boundary sits near 11° at Mach 2 and falls as Mach rises.
  12. Close the RUN VALVE when TANK LOW flashes (40 bar) or your readings are complete. Record run time from the drum.
  13. For further runs, restart the compressor with the heater ON and repeat from step 5. At end of shift: valve SHUT, motor STOP, heater OFF, breaker OFF.

Alarms

Window Meaning Action
TANK LOW Charge below 40 bar while flowing. Shut the run valve; recharge before the next run.
DRYER WET Silica gel saturated past 85%. Heater ON with breaker ON; wait for regeneration.
MOISTURE Condensation forming in the nozzle: stagnation temperature below the dewline marked on the thermometer. Shut down at once or the optics foul. See Faults and recovery.
BUFFET Model shaking on the sting beyond the buffet boundary. Ease incidence immediately; see Faults and recovery.
UNSTART Diffuser has swallowed the shock. Latched. Automatic emergency dump has already shut the valve. Put bleed and incidence right, then TRIP RESET to free the valve.
COMP HOT Compressor motor at or above 95°C, or a winding fault. Stop the motor, cool below 70°C, TRIP RESET.
STING DMG Sting fatigued by sustained buffet. Latched; drive broken. See Faults and recovery.
OPTICS Schlieren windows fouled by condensed moisture. Latched. See Faults and recovery.

Jewels: POWER (white, supply alive), TANKS CHARGED (amber neon, 200 bar), RUNNING (blue, supersonic flow established), FAULT (red, any window standing; flashes until accepted).

Faults and recovery

Nozzle condensation

You see: DRYER WET or MOISTURE showing; a milky fog rolling through the Schlieren view; the stagnation thermometer fallen below its dewline mark. Ignored, the moisture plates itself on the windows and OPTICS latches - the view is lost for the rest of the shift.

Recovery: shut the RUN VALVE. With MAIN BREAKER ON, leave AIR DRYER HEATER ON until DRYER WET goes dark and the gel reads well below saturation. When the dryer has recovered, press TRIP RESET: OPTICS clears and the windows polish up. Warm the charge with the heater before the next run - it exists for exactly this reason. Test the chain with the FAULT TEST SWITCHES MOISTURE INGRESS toggle.

Sting buffet oscillation

You see: BUFFET flashing, the model trembling in the window, the balance needles hunting. Held above the boundary for more than about six seconds the sting fatigues: STING DMG latches, the handwheel dies in your hands and the charge dumps itself through the exhaust.

Recovery: shut the RUN VALVE and bring ANGLE OF ATTACK HANDWHEEL back to zero. Press TRIP RESET: the drive engages again, STING DMG and BUFFET clear. Next run, sweep more slowly and respect the boundary - it falls with Mach. Test the chain with the FAULT TEST SWITCHES STING OSCILLATION toggle; note the fault stands until you stop the flow and reset.

Compressor overheat

You see: COMP HOT showing, motor temperature needle past 95°C, compressor stopped dead by its thermal latch (or seized hot if the overload came from a winding fault). Charging halts until cleared.

Recovery: leave COMPRESSOR MOTOR STOPPED and let the motor cool below 70°C on the thermometer. Then press TRIP RESET, start the motor again and continue charging in shorter bursts - she heats about ninety degrees a minute of hard running. Test the chain with the FAULT TEST SWITCHES MOTOR OVERLOAD toggle.

Diffuser unstart is not a stored fault but it dumps your charge just the same: UNSTART latches and the valve locks. Correct bleed or incidence, then TRIP RESET. COLD RESET returns everything to cold at any point, clearing every alarm, latch and injected fault.
