Overview

This box works the up line of the single-branch with a passing loop at Penarrow station. The frame before you carries six levers at two-and-a-half inch pitch, built on the Company’s standard interlocking pattern. Two stop signals, one distant signal, one facing-point lock, the loop points and two starting signals are worked from this box; the line beyond is worked to Pendower box by single-needle telegraph under the absolute block regulations. Up passenger trains are booked over the main road through the platform; goods and mineral traffic is booked into the loop road, so as to leave the main line free.

The signalman’s duty is plain. Accept the offered train on the bell, set the proper road, give the driver a clear home signal, obtain line clear from Pendower, and start the train away. The interlocking will refuse any movement which would endanger the train; do not seek to defeat it. Every lever you leave over, every minute the train stands, is entered against the shift in the delay register on the shelf.

The first principle of this railway. The frame is so arranged that a wrong action is refused, not forgiven. If a lever will not come out of the frame, something is protecting a train. Find out what, before you try again.

Controls

No. 1 — UP HOME LEVER
Works the up home signal at the facing points. Pull to lower the arm (line clear); the arm returns to danger of its own weight when the engine passes it, and the lever must be put back before it can be pulled again. Locked whenever the point lock is out, the points are undetected, or the road set is occupied.
No. 2 — UP DISTANT LEVER
Works the up distant, read against the home. It can be pulled at any time but shows caution only when No. 1 is off. Put it back when the home is put back.
No. 3 — MAIN STARTER LEVER
Works the starting signal on the main road against the section ahead. Will not come out of the frame unless Pendower has given line clear on the block instrument and the section ahead is clear.
No. 4 — POINT LOCK LEVER
Releases the facing-point lock on points No. 5. Pulled, the lock is out and points No. 5 may be stroked; pushed back, the points are locked and detected. Locked over an occupied line.
No. 5 — LOOP POINTS LEVER
Strokes points No. 5. Normal (lever in frame) the facing lies for the main road; pulled, for the loop road. Moves only when No. 4 is pulled. A full stroke of this lever also re-clutches the point drive after points out of correspondence.
No. 6 — LOOP STARTER LEVER
Works the starting signal on the loop road, subject to line clear as No. 3.
LINE BELL PLUNGER
The single-needle bell to Tregony and Pendower. When the TRAIN OFFERED tile is lit, press once to accept the train. When your train is standing ready and the needle shows LINE BLOCKED, press once to offer forward; Pendower replies and the needle rises to LINE CLEAR. At any other time a single test stroke only.
FAULT TEST SWITCHES (maintenance drawer)
Three guarded switches marked TEST 1, TEST 2 and TEST 3, for use outside traffic hours. They inject, respectively: points out of correspondence, track circuit failure, and signal wire fracture. Switching TEST 3 off effects the repair; the other two faults are cured only by their proper recovery actions described below.
RESEAT TC D plunger (maintenance drawer)
Presses up the dropped relay of track circuit D after track circuit failure. One firm press.
RESTORE FRAME (maintenance drawer)
Returns the whole simulation to cold: all levers normal, all faults cleared, all incidents expunged, the register zeroed. To be used after a derailment or collision, or at the change of shift.

Normal operation

  1. On taking charge, satisfy yourself that every lever is in the frame, the block needle stands at LINE BLOCKED, and no tile on the annunciator is lit.
  2. When the bell rings twice and the TRAIN OFFERED tile lights, note the ticket on the spike: a PASSENGER train requires the MAIN road, a FREIGHT train requires the LOOP road. Press the LINE BELL PLUNGER once to accept. The TRAIN APPROACHING tile follows within half a minute. (If the offer is not accepted within three-quarters of a minute, Tregony will assume acceptance and send the train; four minutes are entered against the shift.)
  3. Set the road. Pull POINT LOCK LEVER (No. 4); stroke LOOP POINTS LEVER (No. 5) for the loop, or leave it in the frame for the main; push No. 4 back in. The diagram blades must agree with the road required.
  4. Pull UP DISTANT LEVER (No. 2) and UP HOME LEVER (No. 1). Both arms fall; the home lamp burns clear. If either lever is locked, read the reason on the prompt plate and remedy it — never force the frame.
  5. When the train has passed the home, that signal returns to danger of its own weight. Put No. 1 and No. 2 back in the frame while the train runs onto the road.
  6. With the train standing on its road, press the LINE BELL PLUNGER once to offer forward. When Pendower replies, the needle rises to LINE CLEAR.
  7. Pull the starter for the road in use — MAIN STARTER LEVER (No. 3) or LOOP STARTER LEVER (No. 6). The train moves away into the section; the starter returns to danger behind it.
  8. When the bell rings thrice and the OUT OF SECTION tile lights, the needle has fallen to LINE BLOCKED. Restore the starter lever, set the frame for the next train, and enter the working in the register.

Alarms

Indication Meaning Action
APPROACH WITHOUT ROUTE A train is running towards a home signal held at danger. Set the road and pull Nos. 2 and 1 at once. If it cannot be done, stand clear: the driver will act under the rules.
PASSED AT DANGER plate on the diagram SIGNAL PASSED AT DANGER: a driver has passed the home at danger after standing ninety seconds. The train creeps at walking pace onto whichever road lies set. Give it the road and start it forward as soon as line clear can be had. The occurrence is reported; it is not erased except by RESTORE FRAME.
WRONG ROAD SET A passenger train has been sent over the loop, or a goods train over the main. Not dangerous, but two minutes are entered against the shift. Re-set the frame correctly for the next train.
PTS WRONG plate on the diagram POINTS NOT DETECTED: see Faults and recovery, item 1. No train may be accepted over undetected points. Recover per item 1.
TC D jewel lit with no train in section TRACK CIRCUIT FAILURE: see Faults and recovery, item 2. Recover per item 2.
U.HOME arm fallen, lever slack SIGNAL WIRE FRACTURE: see Faults and recovery, item 3. Recover per item 3.
COLLISION Two trains have met. The section is fouled. The frame is stopped until the inspector’s reset. Press RESTORE FRAME.
DERAILMENT An engine has run through undetected points at No. 5. The frame is stopped until the inspector’s reset. Press RESTORE FRAME.
DELAYED WORKING The shift has fallen more than a quarter-hour behind the service. Work the sequence smartly; the register forgives nothing.

Faults and recovery

1. Points out of correspondence (clutch slip)

You see: the PTS WRONG plate on the diagram; the prompt reads PTS. No. 5 NOT DETECTED; Nos. 1, 3 and 6 are locked (the interlocking refuses a signal over undetected points).

Recovery:

  1. Be sure no train is between the home signal and the starters; if one is berthed, hold it where it stands.
  2. Pull POINT LOCK LEVER (No. 4) to release the lock.
  3. Give LOOP POINTS LEVER (No. 5) one full stroke — out or in, either way — so that the drive re-clutches.
  4. Push No. 4 back in. The PTS WRONG plate clears; work the traffic as usual.

To practise: switch TEST 1 in the FAULT TEST SWITCHES. Switching it off again does not cure the fault; only a full restroke of No. 5 does.

2. Track circuit failure (section D sticks occupied)

You see: the D track-circuit lamp burns red although the section is visibly empty; the block needle cannot be raised from LINE BLOCKED; both starters are locked; trains pile up behind one another until one passes the home at danger.

Recovery:

  1. Satisfy yourself by eye that the section ahead is truly clear of engines.
  2. Press the RESEAT TC D plunger in the maintenance drawer, one firm press.
  3. The D lamp falls dark; offer forward on the plunger and resume working.

To practise: switch TEST 2 in the FAULT TEST SWITCHES. Only the reseat plunger restores the relay.

3. Signal wire fracture (home arm falls)

You see: the up home arm falls to danger of itself; No. 1 springs back into the frame; the prompt reads U.HOME WIRE SLACK — ARM FALLEN; further pulling of No. 1 is refused.

Recovery:

  1. Hold approaching trains: the approach alarm will sound; expect a stop.
  2. Make the repair: switch TEST 3 of the FAULT TEST SWITCHES off (the wire is re-made and pinned by the linesmen).
  3. Pull UP DISTANT LEVER (No. 2) and UP HOME LEVER (No. 1) afresh; both arms fall and the road may be worked.

To practise: switch TEST 3 on; the fault stands until switched off.

After a collision or derailment

The frame stops entirely — no lever answers, no train moves — until the inspector has been round. Press RESTORE FRAME; the box returns to cold and the register to zero.

Specifications

Frame Six levers, 2½-inch pitch, Company’s standard interlocking pattern; double-wire transmission, 3 to 1 lead
Signal levers Nos. 1, 3 and 6 stop signals (red bands); No. 2 distant (yellow band); weighted spectacles return arms to danger of their own weight
Points No. 5 facing points to the loop, detected by lock bar; facing-point lock engaged by No. 4, released only on a clear line
Track circuits A (approach), B1 (main road), B2 (loop road), D (section ahead); DC relays, primary batteries in the well
Block sections Tregony–Penarrow (up), absolute block, single-needle telegraph, three positions: LINE BLOCKED / LINE CLEAR / TRAIN ON LINE
Bell codes 2 beats train offered; 1 beat acceptance or attention; 3 beats out of section; continuous attention call on failure
Speeds Passenger over main road not above 15 m.p.h. through the station; freight over the loop 10 m.p.h.; a train that has passed a signal at danger proceeds at walking pace
Standing limits An engine held ninety seconds at a signal shown at danger passes it under Rule 93(b); an offer unacknowledged forty-five seconds is taken as accepted
Sighting Distant readable 600 yd in clear weather; home visible 400 yd on the curve; oil luminous burners in the winter months
Register Delay ledger kept on the block shelf; more than fifteen minutes lost invokes the DIVISIONAL INSPECTOR
Maintenance apparatus Three guarded test switches, one relay-reseat plunger, one frame-restoring key; hammer-finish drawer, not to be opened during traffic hours
