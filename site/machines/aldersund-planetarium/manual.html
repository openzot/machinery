Overview

The Typ 63-S is an opto-mechanical star projector: two hardened spheres carrying thirty-one star plates apiece, hung from a polar yoke over the centre of a fourteen-metre plaster dome. A twin carbon arc in the lamp house feeds light to the plates through condensers; drum gear trains carry the whole sky westward at sidereal or solar rate, or twelve times faster for the school parties; a hand crank under the port tips the entire cage to set your latitude between ten and seventy degrees north. The console before you is the whole machine reduced to a bench: power and arc on the left, the sky in the middle, motion and shutter on the right, and the annunciator strip along the bottom to tell you what she wants before she wants it.

She is a showman's instrument, not a laboratory one. Her three tempers are the arc, her air, and her drums: keep the needle in the green field, keep air moving whenever current flows, and give the gears their re-cage at six hours west every run, and she will throw four thousand stars a night for forty years. Neglect any of the three and she will stop the show in front of a hundred and eighty seated guests — the gears first, and they will take an hour of jogging to forgive you.

Controls

HUVUDSTRÖM · MAINS SWITCH
Ivory paddle, left bay. Down is off, up is on. Energises the rheostat motor, blower motor and lamp circuits; her amber pilot burns while she is made. Everything else waits on this switch.
FLÄKT · BLOWER SWITCH
Paddle beside the mains. Runs the rotary blower that cools the arc and clears ozone from the lamp house. On whenever the arc burns; run her sixty seconds after lamp-off before going cold.
BÅGSTRÖM · LAMP CURRENT
Knurled knob with the aluminium ammeter beside it. Sets arc current from nought to forty amperes; the arc strikes above eleven and a half and should be worked onto thirty, where the needle sits in the green field. Below about twenty-six the field flickers; below thirteen the stars fall apart.
DAGSRATEL · RATE SELECTOR
Four-position selector at the head of the right bay. STOP holds the sky; STJÄRNA carries her westward at sidereal rate, one turn in twenty-four hours; SOL at solar rate; DEMO runs twelve times sidereal for school demonstrations. Turn her only through detents, never past them by force.
POLHÖJD · LATITUDE CRANK
The flywheel crank under the dome port, reading on the quadrant scale. Winds the whole star cage over its yoke from ten to seventy degrees north. Crank gently when the drums are running: hard cranking against a moving train loads the gears just as surely as missing the re-cage.
OMKOPPLINGSHEVARM · RE-CAGE LEVER
Guarded lever inside the red collar. CAGE lifts the drum pinions clear and re-indexes them as the hour angle passes six hours west; RUN engages them again. The guard must be lifted before the lever will move. This is the one control on the bench that saves you an hour — use her every run.
KUPELLUCKA · SHUTTER KEYS
Two keys, ÖPPNA and STÄNG. Drive the dome shutter up or down; the little window between them shows OPEN, RISING, LOWERING or CLOSED. Never close her on a burning lamp except in emergency: the trapped heat soaks the plate stack.
SALBELYSNING · HOUSE LIGHTS
Slide dimmer on the right bay. Warms the plaster for walking the audience in and out; full dark for the show itself. She washes the stars out of the port exactly as fast as you would expect.
KVITTERA · ALARM ACCEPT
Red key on the key bench. Stops the flashing on a struck annunciator and sounds-off the gong; the window stays lit while the fault stands, because a light you cannot argue with is the only kind worth having.
PROVA · LAMPS TEST
Key beside accept. Hold her down and every annunciator window and pilot on the console lights. Prove her before every performance; a dead bulb in a test is a trifle, a dead bulb in an emergency is a blind operator.
UNDERHÅLL · FAULT TESTS
Three red keys in the maintenance tray: TEST FLÄKT (blower failure), TEST LJUSBÅGE (arc instability) and TEST DRIVNING (gear train jam). They put the exact working fault onto the machine so you can rehearse her recoveries with the house empty.
BRYTARE · FAN BREAKER
Slate key in the recovery group. Re-arms the blower breaker after it has tripped. She is interlocked: rest the blower switch to OFF first, press the breaker, then switch on again.
LOSSNING · JOG RELEASE
Slate key, hold-to-operate. With the drums stopped and the re-cage lever at CAGE, holding her walks the strained gear train gently free. Watch the strain fall on the DRUM STRAIN window's neighbours: jog until the load rests, then trip reset.
LOSS SPÄRR · TRIP RESET
Slate key in the recovery group. Clears the lamp thermal latch after an overtemp, and releases the drum drive after a gear jam once the strain has been jogged out. She refuses while the train is still bound.
NOLLSTÄLL · CONSOLE RESET
Key at the end of the bench. Returns the whole machine to cold: switches open, current at zero, shutter down, alarms dark, faults cleared. For the lecturer who inherits the bench mid-shift, not for use during a show.

Normal operation

  1. Cold bench check. Press PROVA · LAMPS TEST and hold: every window, pilot and key legend lights. Release. All annunciators dark, all paddles down, current knob fully left, shutter showing CLOSED.
  2. Throw HUVUDSTRÖM · MAINS SWITCH up. Her amber pilot lights and the rheostat motor hums ready.
  3. Throw FLÄKT · BLOWER SWITCH up and watch the airflow strip climb. Do nothing else until she stands above ninety per cent.
  4. Ease BÅGSTRÖM · LAMP CURRENT round until the ammeter passes eleven and a half and the arc strikes, then walk the needle onto thirty amperes, into the green field. The thermometer climbs to her working warmth around sixty degrees and rests there.
  5. Set SALBELYSNING · HOUSE LIGHTS down toward dark as the audience settles, and press ÖPPNA. Watch the shutter state rise RISING to OPEN; the stars come up faintly as the lamp reaches temperature.
  6. Turn DAGSRATEL · RATE SELECTOR to STJÄRNA. The sky begins her westward walk; the sidereal drum under the port turns with her.
  7. Before the CAGE AT SIX window lights — six hours west — bring the selector to STOP, lift the red guard, move OMKOPPLINGSHEVARM · RE-CAGE LEVER to CAGE and back to RUN, close the guard, and return the selector to STJÄRNA. The drums re-index themselves in the pause. On DEMO nights this comes round every twenty seconds; either slow the demonstration or stop for each passage.
  8. To change latitude between numbers, stop the drums first, crank POLHÖJD · LATITUDE CRANK watching the quadrant pointer, and start the drums again. Polaris rides at your latitude's height on the north meridian; use her to sanity-check the setting.
  9. End of show: RATE SELECTOR to STOP, STÄNG to lower the shutter, and only when she shows CLOSED, take LAMP CURRENT to zero. Run the blower a full minute on the cooling lamp, switch her off, and drop HUVUDSTRÖM · MAINS SWITCH. Cover the port bezel with its bag and log the arc hours in the front of this book.

Alarms

ARC OVERTEMP · ÖVERVÄRME
Lamp metal past one hundred and four degrees. Expect the thermal latch at one hundred and twelve: the arc breaks herself rather than cook the plates. Action: current to zero at once, prove the blower strip, let her cool below eighty, then LOSS SPÄRR · TRIP RESET and relight.
AIR LOSS · LUFTBORTFALL
The blower breaker has tripped, or demanded air is not arriving. Current without air is the one combination this machine will not survive quietly. Action: lamp current to zero, rest the blower switch, press the FAN BREAKER, restore the blower, watch her climb, then relight the arc.
ARC UNSTABLE · LJUSBÅGE
The arc is hunting: the stars flicker and the stability falls. Usually the trim has drifted off the green field. Action: ease LAMP CURRENT onto thirty and hold her there a few seconds; the arc settles and the window goes dark by herself. If she drops below a third stability for twelve seconds the arc breaks and TRIP RESET is wanted after re-trimming.
DRUM STRAIN · DRIVPÅDRAG
The gear trains are being loaded: a missed re-cage, hard cranking against a moving sky, or a fault test. Action: selector to STOP, lever to CAGE, and the strain drains away. Above forty-five she shouts; ignore her to one hundred and see GEAR JAM.
CAGE AT SIX · OMKOPPLA SÖDER
Advisory, amber. You are within twenty-seven minutes of hour of the six-hours-west passage with the drums running uncaged. Action: perform the re-cage ritual from step seven of Normal operation. She is a courtesy. Missing her is not.
HEAT SOAK · LUKEN STÄNGD
The shutter is closed or nearly so while the arc carries current: heat is pooling in the lamp house. Action: open the shutter, or if the dome must stay shut for weather, take the current to zero and end the show honestly.
GEAR JAM · DRIVSPÄRR
The strain passed one hundred and the train has bound. The drive locks and no selector position will move her. Action: selector to STOP, lift the guard, lever to CAGE, hold LOSSNING · JOG RELEASE until the load rests, then LOSS SPÄRR · TRIP RESET, lever to RUN, and resume gently. Log her in the book; two jams in a month means the works are called, not the lecturer.

Faults and recovery

blower failure
You see: AIR LOSS struck immediately with the fan-breaker flag standing red over the annunciator strip; the airflow strip sags toward zero whatever the switch does, and if the arc was burning, ARC OVERTEMP follows within the minute.
Why: the blower motor breaker has tripped — bearing dust, a tired start relay, or the test key.
Clearing: LAMP CURRENT to zero. FLÄKT · BLOWER SWITCH down. Press BRYTARE · FAN BREAKER once; her red stand goes dark. Blower switch up and let the strip climb past ninety. TRIP RESET if the thermal latch had fired, then relight the arc per steps four to six. Prove her with TEST FLÄKT before the next house.
arc instability
You see: ARC UNSTABLE lit and the star field shivering in the port; the ammeter needle trembles though the trim knob has not moved.
Why: the carbons have cratered unevenly or the trim point drifted; the arc hunts across the gap instead of sitting on it.
Clearing: ease BÅGSTRÖM · LAMP CURRENT onto thirty — into the green field — and hold her within an ampere there for six seconds. The hunt dies, the window clears by itself, and the stars steady. If the arc has broken entirely, TRIP RESET and relight. If she hunts again tonight, change the carbon pair at the next interval and say nothing to the lecturers.
gear train jam
You see: DRUM STRAIN climbing through forty-five with the sky still turning, then GEAR JAM, and the heavens freeze solid wherever they were; DEMO nights reach this fastest, one missed passage every twenty seconds.
Why: the drum pinions engaged past six hours west without their re-cage, or the cage was cranked hard against a running train; the teeth have loaded to a bind.
Clearing: RATE SELECTOR to STOP. Lift the guard and put OMKOPPLINGSHEVARM · RE-CAGE LEVER to CAGE. Hold LOSSNING · JOG RELEASE — the train creeps, the load draining — until the strain rests at zero. Press LOSS SPÄRR · TRIP RESET to release the drive lock, return the lever to RUN, close the guard, and resume at STJÄRNA, never straight to DEMO. Full ritual, twice, on TEST DRIVNING with the house empty, before you are trusted with her alone.
