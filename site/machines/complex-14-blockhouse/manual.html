Overview

The Type GM-412/S console commands the terminal phase of a missile launch from inside the reinforced blockhouse: pad clearing, service-arm withdrawal, liquid-oxygen and RP-1 transfer, helium pressurisation, inertial-guidance alignment, and the automatic sequence that runs the count from T−180 seconds through engine start, liftoff and orbital insertion.

The console is operated by two men — the sequencer officer and the propellant officer — but one trained operator may run the drill alone. The machine is deliberately interlocked: the count will not pass an unsafe condition. It holds itself, aborts itself, and waits to be told why. Your work between those events is the drill this manual teaches: load her, pressurise her, align her, start the sequence, and answer every red lamp before the clock answers for you.

A wall periscope beside the desk shows the pad looking east. The count clock above the periscope is the master clock; nothing else on the panel tells time.

Controls

Control Type Positions / action
CONTROL BUS KEY Key switch OFF / ON. Energises the console and all sequencing logic.
PAD CLEAR KEY Key switch OUT / CLEAR. Certifies the pad evacuated; required for transfer and count.
SERVICE ARMS Bat toggle CONNECT / RETRACT. Retract before sequence start; the count auto-holds at T−60 if either arm is still mated.
LAMPS TEST Push Lights every annunciator window, jewel and clock segment. Press again to release.
PROPELLANT SELECTOR Rotary, 4-position OFF / LOAD / TOP OFF / PRESS. LOAD and TOP OFF transfer propellant (pad clear required); PRESS builds ullage pressure from the helium bottle.
LOX VENT VALVE Bat toggle OPEN / SHUT. Open while loading to carry boil-off; shut before PRESS or the tanks will never take pressure.
DELUGE PUMP Bat toggle RUN / OFF. Builds flame-deflector water pressure; ignition is inhibited below 50 psi.
HELIUM REPRESS Guarded push Lift the cover and press for a 2-second emergency helium pulse into both tanks. Each pulse draws roughly 110 psi from the bottle.
GUIDANCE MODE Rotary, 4-position OFF / WARM UP / ALIGN / FLY. Warm-up takes 5 seconds minimum; ALIGN drives alignment to 100 pct at 4 pct per second; FLY hands the loop to the vehicle.
GUIDANCE RESET Push Restarts the computer cold: alignment falls to zero, warm-up restarts. Used after a computer dropout.
RECYCLE SELECTOR Rotary, 4-position RUN / T−180 / T−60 / T−31. Turning to a mark rewinds the clock to that mark and returns the sequencer to READY. No recycle in flight.
COUNT HOLD Push Stops the clock at the displayed second; press again to resume a manual hold. Interlock holds release themselves when their cause clears.
SEQUENCE START Red-collared key Lift the collar and press to run the GO check and start the automatic sequence. A NO-GO condition is named on the clock caption line.
ABORT RESET Push Clears the AUTO ABORT latch once the causing failure is corrected; returns the sequencer to READY at the selected mark.
ALARM ACK Push Acknowledges the annunciator wave: stops the flash and the horn. Lamps stay lit until the cause clears.
PANEL RESET Push Returns the entire installation to its cold, alarm-free state without removing power from the room.
FAULT TEST SWITCHES Guarded toggle bank Lift the striped cover. Three switches inject service failures for drills: PRESS LOSS, GUID DROP, DELUGE LOSS. Deselect after each drill.

Normal Operation

Cold start to orbital insertion. Times assume nominal rates and no test faults.

  1. Turn CONTROL BUS KEY to ON. BUS ENERGISED lights; the clock reads T−03:00.
  2. Turn PROPELLANT SELECTOR to LOAD, open LOX VENT VALVE (lever up), and turn PAD CLEAR KEY to CLEAR. Transfer begins; watch LOX LOAD and RP-1 LOAD climb.
  3. While the tanks load, start DELUGE PUMP (RUN) and let deflector pressure build past 85 psi. DELUGE READY lights.
  4. When RP-1 reaches 98.0 pct, turn PROPELLANT SELECTOR to TOP OFF and let LOX creep onto 99.0 pct or better.
  5. Shut LOX VENT VALVE, then turn PROPELLANT SELECTOR to PRESS. Bring LOX ULLAGE into the green band (14–22 psi) and RP-1 ULLAGE likewise (11–19 psi).
  6. Set GUIDANCE MODE to WARM UP, press GUIDANCE RESET, wait 5 seconds, then set ALIGN. Hold alignment at 100 pct; the meter carries a green band above 95.
  7. Throw SERVICE ARMS to RETRACT. ARMS RETRACTED lights; both swing arms swing clear on the periscope.
  8. Check the board: every status jewel green, no red windows, clock at MARK T−180 READY. Lift the red collar and press SEQUENCE START. The caption reads TERMINAL COUNT RUNNING.
  9. Mind the count. The sequencer auto-holds at T−60 for connected arms and at T−31 for unaligned guidance; it aborts itself at T−3.5 if ullage pressure is lost. Answer any window before continuing.
  10. At T−03 the engines light. Liftoff follows at T+00:02.5. Watch ALT and VEL on the down-range counter and the vehicle climb away on the periscope.
  11. At about T+02:45 insertion is confirmed: the clock caption reads INSERTION CONFIRMED and SEQUENCE burns green. The drill is complete; press PANEL RESET to make the console cold again.

Alarms

Window Colour Meaning Action
TANK PRESSURE LOW Red A loaded tank cannot hold ullage pressure. Select PRESS (vent shut); if pressure still falls, lift the HELIUM REPRESS cover and pulse. Do not enter the last minute below limits.
HELIUM RESERVE LOW Amber Bottle below 800 psi. Stop avoidable draw (leave PRESS only as long as needed). If the bottle empties mid-count, expect the abort.
GUIDANCE FAILURE Red Computer heartbeat lost or alignment decayed. Before start: GUIDANCE RESET, warm-up, realign, resume. In flight: nothing — see Faults and recovery.
ARM INTERLOCK Amber Count holding at T−60 with a service arm mated. Throw SERVICE ARMS to RETRACT; the count resumes by itself.
DELUGE WATER LOSS Red Deflector water below usable pressure. Confirm DELUGE PUMP RUN; ignition is inhibited below 50 psi.
AUTO ABORT Red The sequencer has cut the count (latch set). Note the caption reason, correct the cause, then ABORT RESET, recycle, and start over.
RANGE DESTRUCT Red The vehicle has been destroyed by range safety. None recoverable. Acknowledge the horn; PANEL RESET makes the board cold for the next drill.

The diamond flag at the right end of the annunciator strip rings with the horn while an unacknowledged alarm stands. Press ALARM ACK to silence; the windows stay lit while their causes stand.

Faults and recovery

Inject these only through the guarded FAULT TEST SWITCHES during blockhouse drills. Each is written up exactly as the panel presents it.

tank pressurisation loss

You see: TANK PRESSURE LOW and HELIUM RESERVE LOW; both ullage gauges sag despite the selector at PRESS; the bottle needle drains abnormally fast. If it stands at T−3.5 the count aborts itself (AUTO ABORT).

To clear: deselect the PRESS LOSS test switch (regulator restored), keep the vent shut, select PRESS, and nurse both gauges back into their green bands — use HELIUM REPRESS pulses if the bottle is low. When pressures hold, press ABORT RESET, recycle with RECYCLE SELECTOR to T−60 or T−31, and re-run SEQUENCE START. If the bottle is spent, PANEL RESET restores it.

guidance computer dropout

You see: GUIDANCE FAILURE; the alignment meter collapses; a running count holds itself at T−31. If the computer dies after liftoff the attitude error grows and range safety destroys the vehicle (RANGE DESTRUCT).

To clear: deselect the GUID DROP test switch, press GUIDANCE RESET, set GUIDANCE MODE to WARM UP, wait out the 5-second warm-up, select ALIGN, ride the meter to 95 pct or better, set FLY, and resume or restart the count. There is no recovery in flight; acknowledge the horn and PANEL RESET.

deluge water loss

You see: DELUGE WATER LOSS; the deflector pressure gauge runs away to zero even with the pump running; ignition is inhibited near T−04 and the count aborts.

To clear: cycle DELUGE PUMP OFF then RUN after deselecting the DELUGE LOSS test switch; let pressure rebuild past 85 psi, confirm DELUGE READY lit, press ABORT RESET, recycle to T−31 or T−60, and start the sequence again.
