Overview

The Osprey Mk.IV is a seven-cylinder air-cooled radial aero engine of 1,530 cubic inches, rated 275 brake horsepower at 2,250 rev/min and +9 lb/in² boost. Before any engine is passed for dispatch it runs its acceptance schedule on Bed No. 2, coupled to a water-brake dynamometer in the closed cell adjoining this console.

The console puts the whole bed in front of one man: fuel, ignition, throttle and mixture on the left and centre; oil, head temperature, fuel flow and dynamometer load on the right. The engine is started on the inertia starter — wind her flywheel with SPIN, then pull MESH — and she is warmed, checked and loaded by hand. She will bite the careless: an ignored oil temperature lamp will seize the big end inside two minutes at rated power, and a flooded float chamber will drown her out altogether. Read Section 5 before you first press SPIN.

Controls

MASTER SWITCH
Battery and bus for the whole console and starter. UP is on.
FUEL PUMP
Aircraft fuel pump motor. The engine cannot run or start without it.
PRIMER
Semi-rotary hand primer. Each stroke charges the induction system. A cold engine wants two strokes; a warm restart wants none.
STARTER SPIN
Hold down to wind the inertia flywheel. Wind past 1,400 rev/min on the FLYWHEEL dial before meshing; below 750 she will not turn the compressor over compression.
STARTER MESH
Pull knob. Throws the spinning flywheel into the ring gear; she turns for about three seconds. If she has not fired, spin again.
MAGNETO SELECTOR
OFF — L — R — BOTH. Run on BOTH except during the magneto check. OFF stops the engine.
THROTTLE
Quadrant lever, closed at the bottom. Crack half an inch for starting.
THROTTLE FRICTION
Small knurled clamp beside the quadrant. Nip it to hold the lever where you set it; slacken before moving the lever.
MIXTURE
IDLE CUT-OFF — AUTO LEAN — AUTO RICH. Start and run at RICH; CUT-OFF is the normal stopping position.
COOLING GILLS
Cowl flap lever, shut to open across the slot. The first defence against both temperature lamps.
DYNO LOAD
Water-brake control, SHUT to FULL. Load is taken up only after warm-up.
CARB DRAIN
Red push under a yellow guard. Hold to drain the float chamber while the master switch is on.
ALARM SILENCE
Kills the alarm bell until a new fault comes up. It clears nothing.
LAMPS TEST
Lights every jewel on the strip. Prove it before every run.
TEST BED RESET
Returns the whole bench to cold: switches out, levers shut, lamps dark, faults cleared. For use after a seizure or at shift end.

Normal operation

  1. Console cold: check THROTTLE fully closed and nipped with THROTTLE FRICTION, MIXTURE at IDLE CUT-OFF, MAGNETO SELECTOR at OFF, DYNO LOAD at SHUT, COOLING GILLS shut.
  2. Press LAMPS TEST and satisfy yourself that all eight jewels burn; press again.
  3. MASTER SWITCH up. FUEL PUMP on. Listen for the pump.
  4. MIXTURE to AUTO RICH. Crack the THROTTLE half an inch; re-nip the friction clamp.
  5. MAGNETO SELECTOR to BOTH.
  6. Two strokes on the PRIMER if the engine is cold.
  7. Hold STARTER SPIN until the FLYWHEEL dial passes 1,400, then pull STARTER MESH smartly.
  8. When she fires, ease the THROTTLE to 550–600 rev/min and confirm OIL PRESS comes up off the bottom within ten seconds. If it does not, stop on the MIXTURE and investigate.
  9. Warm at 1,100 rev/min until OIL TEMP reads 40 °C or better. Use the COOLING GILLS sparingly while warming.
  10. Magneto check at 1,300 rev/min: L, then R, then BOTH. A drop over 150 rev/min, or a rough change, ends the run — see Section 5.
  11. Take DYNO LOAD to about 60 per cent, open the THROTTLE to 2,250 rev/min and +9 boost, and hold the rated run five minutes. Keep OIL TEMP under 112 °C with the COOLING GILLS; B.H.P. should stand near the 275 mark.
  12. Cooling trial: THROTTLE to idle, DYNO LOAD shut, GILLS wide, two minutes at 1,000 rev/min.
  13. To stop: DYNO LOAD SHUT, run two minutes at 800 rev/min, MIXTURE to IDLE CUT-OFF, then MAGNETO SELECTOR OFF and MASTER SWITCH down when the propeller stops.

Alarms

Lamp Meaning Action
STARTER (amber) Flywheel winding or crank in progress. None; goes out when the crank finishes.
ROUGH RUN (amber) Erratic combustion: earthed magneto in circuit, or flooding. Read the dials; treat under Section 5 before touching the throttle again.
HEAD HOT (red) Cylinder heads at 232 °C or more. As for OIL TEMP HOT. Ignoring either lamp seizes the big end.
OIL PRESS LOW (red) Under 38 lb/in² above 450 rev/min. Throttle back. If pressure does not recover, MIXTURE CUT-OFF and do not restart.
CARB FLOOD (red) Float chamber above a third full. Drain per Section 5 before any attempt to spin.
SEIZURE (red) Big end seized. The bed is out of service. Nothing. Press TEST BED RESET to bar the bed ready for the next engine.

The bell rings while any new alarm stands. ALARM SILENCE quiets it until the next fresh fault; it does not acknowledge the condition away.

Faults and recovery

Magneto earthing fault

You see: MAGNETO EARTH FAULT on the annunciator; running, the ROUGH RUN lamp with her, a flapping needle on REV/MIN and a loss of boost. Imposed for drill with the EARTHED MAG switch under the FAULT TEST SWITCHES cover.

Recovery: put the MAGNETO SELECTOR to L, which isolates the earthed right magneto. The fault lamp goes out and she runs sweet, if short of her rated power; log the drop and make for landing attenuation. Full clearance: return the EARTHED MAG switch to DOWN, or press TEST BED RESET.

Oil cooler blockage

You see: OIL COOLER BLOCKED announced, and with the engine loaded the OIL TEMP climbs through its amber arc however far the gills are opened. Imposed with the BLOCKED COOLER switch.

Recovery: COOLING GILLS fully open, DYNO LOAD shut, and throttle back to 1,500 rev/min or less. Watch OIL TEMP fall below 105 °C before loading again, and then keep her at part power. Full clearance: when she is cool, return the BLOCKED COOLER switch to DOWN, or press TEST BED RESET.

Float chamber flooding

You see: CARB FLOOD on the strip, FUEL flow high and rising, black smoke at the stack, then ROUGH RUN; left alone she drowns at about nine-tenths full and stops dead. Imposed with the FLOODED FLOAT switch, and met honestly in service after a hard priming.

Recovery: lift the guard and hold CARB DRAIN with the MASTER SWITCH on; take the MIXTURE to IDLE CUT-OFF so she cannot fire on the rich charge. Watch the level fall on the condition of the lamp; when CARB FLOOD goes out, drain another five seconds, restore the MIXTURE to RICH and re-start by Section 3 if she has stopped. Full clearance: return the FLOODED FLOAT switch to DOWN, or press TEST BED RESET.

Seizure

You see: a grinding cough, REV/MIN and OIL PRESS falling together, and the SEIZURE lamp latched red. This is not recovered; it is atoned for. The engine comes off the bed for strip-down and TEST BED RESET bars the console cold for the next engine.

Specifications

Engine - Osprey Mk.IV radial, air-cooled, 7 cylinders
Bore × stroke 146 mm × 140 mm (5¾ × 5½ in)
Swept volume 1,530 cu in (25.1 litres)
Rated power 275 bhp at 2,250 rev/min, +9 lb/in² boost
Take-off power 340 bhp at 2,400 rev/min, +12 lb/in² boost (one minute)
Idling speed 550 rev/min minimum, 600 recommended warm
Red-line rev/min 2,600 (overspeed ring begins 2,600 on the tachometer)
Oil pressure, running 60–75 lb/in²; alarm below 38
Oil inlet temperature Nominal 85 °C; amber 85–112; alarm at 112; seizure risk beyond 138
Cylinder head temperature Nominal 165–190 °C; amber above 190; alarm at 232
Fuel consumption at rated About 55 gal/hr, auto rich
Inertia starter Mesh above 750 flywheel rev/min; wind to 1,400–1,650
Dynamometer Water brake, continuous duty to 300 bhp absorption
Schedule time, cold to release Not less than 45 minutes
