Overview

This manual covers the VINDSKÆR V-62 nacelle service controller, type 6210, as fitted to the 1300 kW grid turbine of the same name. The controller is the door panel inside the tower head: it starts and stops the machine, trims the blades and the nacelle by hand for service work, secures the rotor on the mechanical lock pin, watches the drive train, and stands between the North Sea wind and the 690 V grid.

The V-62 is a pitch-regulated, three-blade machine. Above rated wind (13 m/s) the controller spills surplus gusts by feathering the blades a few degrees; above the cut-out (24 m/s) it shuts itself down in an orderly way. Everything on the panel is keyed or interlocked because the person standing in front of it is usually alone, ninety metres up, with one hand occupied.

ADVARSEL / WARNING. Work inside the nacelle only with the key at STOP, the hydraulic pump off, the ROTOR BRAKE applied and the ROTOR LOCK PIN fully engaged (tag reading INDSATT · IN). Never release the brake with the pin withdrawn unless the blades are feathered and nobody is near the hub.

Controls

Control Type Action and positions
MODE KEY 3-position key switch STOP — everything released, blades spring to feather, brake applied. SERVICE — jogs and pumps enabled, grid closed interlocked out. AUTO — automatic running; the GRID CONTACTOR may close. The key cycles through the three positions in order.
HYD PUMP Illuminated toggle Runs the 160 bar power pack for brake, pitch accumulator and lock pin circuits. Works only out of STOP. Pressure shows on the bar gauge; expect full pressure in about ten seconds.
ROTOR LOCK PIN Spring pin with yellow tag Engages the mechanical rotor lock. Withdraw (UDTAGET · OUT) only when the rotor is stopped and the brake is applied; re-engage before leaving the nacelle.
ROTOR BRAKE Guarded toggle Applies (I) or releases (O) the disc brake. Needs more than 90 bar to develop full torque; below that it only damps. Lift the yellow guard flap to reach the lever.
GRID CONTACTOR Pushbutton under hinged cover Closes the turbine to the network (AUTO mode only, lock pin out, no trips). Press again to open. The converter pre-charges below 12 rpm, so closing during spool-up is safe.
PITCH JOG Twin rocker Feathers (▲) or flattens (FINE ▼) the blades in 4° steps, SERVICE mode only. Watch the PITCH figure in the mimic diagram.
YAW JOG CW / CCW push pair Slewes the nacelle 6° per press, SERVICE mode only. Every degree of yaw winds or unwinds the loop cable: watch the TWIST counter.
COOLING SELECTOR Rotary selector OFF — oil cooler stopped. AUTO — cooler follows gearbox temperature. FORCED — cooler runs continuously, used when the oil runs hot.
SENSOR HEATER Illuminated toggle Warms the anemometer and wind vane. About forty seconds of heating clears rime ice from the sensors.
ALARM ACK Grey pushbutton Acknowledges new alarms: the flashing window stops flashing and the horn falls silent. An alarm that is still present stays lit steady.
LAMP TEST Black pushbutton Lights every LED, annunciator window and stack-light lamp while held for two seconds.
SERVICE TESTS Bay of three test buttons under cover Injects a test fault — ANEMO (anemometer icing), OLIE (gearbox oil overheating) or TVIST (yaw cable twist) — so the technician can prove the alarms and the recovery drills without waiting for winter.
RESET Blue pushbutton Clears a latched trip once its cause has gone: rotor below 23.5 rpm after OVERSPEED, oil below 75 °C, twist unwound below 300°. Returns the key logic to STOP.
EMERGENCY STOP Red mushroom on yellow collar Opens the contactor, feathers the blades and latches an EMERGENCY STOP trip. Twist to release, then press RESET before restarting.

Normal operation

  1. Confirm the LCD shows MODE:STOP and the stack light is dark. Turn the MODE KEY one step to SERVICE.
  2. Switch HYD PUMP on and watch the hydraulic gauge climb into its green arc (about 160 bar).
  3. With the rotor stationary and the brake lever at I, withdraw the ROTOR LOCK PIN; the tag must read UDTAGET · OUT.
  4. Lift the guard on the ROTOR BRAKE and release the brake (lever O). The rotor may idle slowly against the generator windage.
  5. Turn the MODE KEY to AUTO. The blades trim to fine and the rotor soft-spools to roughly 13 rpm idling speed; the LCD shows KLAR - CLOSE CONTACTOR.
  6. Raise the cover and press GRID CONTACTOR. The converter pre-charges, the rotor comes up to 19 rpm and the kW readout climbs. The LCD confirms PÅ NETTET / ON GRID and the stack light turns green.
  7. Set the COOLING SELECTOR to AUTO and check the gearbox oil gauge settles between 55 and 75 °C.
  8. Leave the machine in AUTO. Use PITCH JOG, YAW JOG or the test bay only with the key at SERVICE.
  9. To shut down: press GRID CONTACTOR again (or turn the key to STOP), let the rotor stop, apply the ROTOR BRAKE, switch the HYD PUMP off, re-engage the ROTOR LOCK PIN and return the key to STOP.

Alarms

Window Meaning Immediate action
OVERSPEED Rotor above 23.5 rpm. Automatic feathering begins; at 26.5 rpm the machine trips. Press ALARM ACK, stand clear, let the trip do its work. After RESET investigate pitch response before restarting.
OIL TEMP Gearbox oil above 82 °C. Trip follows at 95 °C. COOLING SELECTOR to FORCED, shed load (feather via SERVICE), watch for the fall below 78 °C.
HYD PRESS Pressure below 115 bar with the pump expected to run. Check HYD PUMP is on. Below 90 bar the brake cannot hold: feather first, then brake.
TWIST Loop cable wound past ±540°. Key to SERVICE and unwind with YAW JOG until the counter reads under ±300°.
ANEMO Anemometer disagreeing with the machine — typically rime ice. Switch SENSOR HEATER on for at least forty seconds. Until the reading recovers the governor is blind: feather early. Unchecked blindness ends in a SENSOR FAIL trip.
TRIP A protection has latched the machine out (see LCD line 1 for the cause). Clear the cause, then RESET. The LCD names the trip: OVERSPEED, GEARBOX TEMP, TWIST LIMIT or EMERGENCY STOP.

New alarms flash and sound the horn; ALARM ACK steadies them. A window that stays lit after acknowledgement still demands attention before the next start.

Faults and recovery

Anemometer icing

You see: the wind gauge frozen low or falling although the rotor behaves as if blown hard; ANEMO flashing; in AUTO the governor holds the blades too fine because it believes the wind has dropped — the road to OVERSPEED.
To clear: switch SENSOR HEATER on. After about forty heated seconds the reading jumps back to the true wind and the alarm drops out. If the machine has already tripped on overspeed, wait for the rotor to slow below 23.5 rpm, press RESET, and repeat the start-up procedure.

Gearbox oil overheating

You see: the oil gauge climbing fast and far — the cooler appears not to cope; OIL TEMP flashing above 82 °C; a GEARBOX TEMP trip follows at 95 °C.
To clear: set the COOLING SELECTOR to FORCED and take load off (feather with PITCH JOG in SERVICE, or accept the controlled shutdown). When the oil falls below 78 °C the fault clears itself; a machine that has tripped may be reset once the oil is below 75 °C.

Yaw cable twist

You see: the TWIST counter winding up steadily; TWIST flashing beyond ±540°; at ±720° a TWIST LIMIT trip stops the machine — the cable would soon be wrung like a dishcloth.
To clear: key to SERVICE and jog YAW JOG against the winding direction until the counter reads within ±300°; the fault then releases. After a TWIST LIMIT trip unwind below 300° and press RESET.

All three faults can be reheated on demand from the SERVICE TESTS bay: ANEMO injects anemometer icing, OLIE injects gearbox oil overheating, TVIST injects yaw cable twist. Prove each alarm and its recovery drill after every control system intervention.
