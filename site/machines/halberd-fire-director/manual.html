Overview

The Director is a computing instrument. Its duty is to watch a moving ship through its optical head, measure her bearing and range continuously, and convert those measurements — allowing for the time of flight of the projectile — into firing orders for the twin 6-inch guns of the battery.

It does not think for itself. The drums will follow any fool at the wheels; they will also follow a drifting gyro or a sagging generator without complaint. The operator's craft is to keep the reticle on the target, to know when the drums are lying, and to put right what the test panel shows broken before the next salvo goes out on false data.

Controls

MOTOR-GENERATOR SWITCH
Guarded toggle under a wire guard, power station, left of panel. ON starts the motor-generator set and energises the 415-volt bus. OFF stops it; three seconds at rest also resets an overloaded breaker.
Gyro voltmeter
Dial instrument beside the switch. Red sector below 350 volts: do not trust the drums there. Needle should stand near V on the scale.
GYRO CAGE SWITCH
Guarded toggle in the computer station. FREE is the working position. CAGE locks the gyroscope against precession; hold CAGE for at least three seconds to re-cage after drift, then return to FREE.
MODE SELECTOR
Bakelite knob with three legend positions beneath it. OFF: drums parked, servo re-syncing. TRACK: tracker live, quality building while you match. COMPUTE: orders flow when quality and gyro conditions are satisfied.
AZIMUTH HANDWHEEL
Nickelled spoked wheel, left of the tracking bench. Turn to train the optical head onto the ship until she sits under the vertical wire of the reticle.
RANGE HANDWHEEL
Twin of the training wheel, right of the bench. Turn to keep the waterline of the ship on the horizontal wire. When both wires hold her, the amber ON TARGET jewel lights.
FIRE KEY
Brass plunger in a red collar, gunnery station. Fires one salvo from both guns, but only when the ORDERS VALID jewel is lit and the barrel-heat gauge is out of the red. Each round adds heat; the liner takes minutes, not seconds, to cool.
ALARM SILENCE
Small black push-button in the annunciator strip. Stops the buzzer and steadies the flashing windows. It silences nothing else: a lit window stays lit until its cause is cleared.
GYRO DRIFT TEST
GENERATOR OVERLOAD TEST
RANGE SERVO TEST
Three guarded toggles behind the MAINTENANCE plate on the bottom rail. They put the named defect onto the machine exactly as action would. Use them in training until recovery is routine; leave them OFF at all other times.
SYSTEM RESET
Ivory button at the end of the maintenance plate. Returns the whole director to cold: power off, drums parked, alarms dark, faults cleared, guns cold.

Normal operation

  1. Inspect that all fault-test toggles are OFF and the MODE SELECTOR stands at OFF. Switch MOTOR-GENERATOR SWITCH to ON.
  2. Watch the voltmeter climb to about 415 volts and wait for the green GYRO READY jewel — roughly twenty seconds of spin-up. Do not hurry this; the drums cannot compute on a lazy flywheel.
  3. Set the MODE SELECTOR to TRACK.
  4. Take station at the wheels. Turn the AZIMUTH HANDWHEEL until the ship rides the vertical wire; turn the RANGE HANDWHEEL until her waterline sits on the horizontal wire. She will not stay put — anticipate her.
  5. When the amber ON TARGET jewel lights and stays lit, hold the match. The quality of your track is building; five steady seconds is enough.
  6. Set the MODE SELECTOR to COMPUTE. The solution drums run up to gun bearing, quadrant elevation and predicted range, and the ORDERS VALID jewel lights.
  7. Check the interlock note reads ORDERS FLOWING. Press the FIRE KEY once per ordered salvo.
  8. Watch BARREL HEAT after each round. If it enters the red sector, cease fire and let it fall below the mark before resuming.

Alarms

Window Meaning Action
LOW VOLTAGE Bus below 380 volts — overloaded set, tripped breaker, or the overload defect itself. Orders are already invalid. Put the M-G switch OFF, count three, ON again. If the window relights, shed everything non-essential and send for the electrical artificer.
GYRO DRIFT The gyroscope is precessing; the bearing drum follows the drift and lays wide. Orders are plausible and wrong. Throw GYRO CAGE SWITCH to CAGE, hold three seconds with the bus healthy, return to FREE, and re-establish your match before trusting the drums.
RANGE SERVO The range-data transmission has jammed; the range counter will not answer its wheel. Stop ranging. Set MODE SELECTOR to OFF, leave it there five seconds for the servo to re-sync, then resume at TRACK.
OVERHEAT Barrel heat has passed 100 on the gauge. The interlock holds fire automatically. Cease fire entirely. Do not touch the key until the gauge falls below the cool mark and the window goes dark by itself.
TRACK LOST In COMPUTE the match has been held too poorly for too long; the plot is stale. Go back to TRACK, re-match on the wheels, and only then return to COMPUTE. Firing on a stale plot wastes the magazine.

Faults and recovery

  1. gyro drift. You see: GYRO DRIFT window lit; the gun-bearing drum walks slowly off the ship even though ON TARGET is lit. Do: confirm bus volts above the red sector; throw the GYRO CAGE SWITCH to CAGE and hold it three full seconds; release to FREE; re-match on the wheels until ON TARGET burns steady; return to COMPUTE and watch the bearing drum settle back. If drift returns within the hour, the gyro is due the workshop — report it.
  2. generator overload. You see: the voltmeter falls into the red sector, lamps dim, LOW VOLTAGE lights, ORDERS VALID goes out. Ignore it long enough and the breaker trips the set completely. Do: MOTOR-GENERATOR SWITCH to OFF; wait three seconds by the sweep of the second hand (less accomplishes nothing); switch ON; verify 415 volts and GYRO READY before recomputing.
  3. range transmission failure. You see: RANGE SERVO window lit; the range handwheel spins free but the range counter and the ship's image along the horizontal wire refuse to move. Do: stop ranging at once; set MODE SELECTOR to OFF for five seconds to let the servo re-sync; the RANGE SERVO window drops; resume at TRACK, rebuild the match, then COMPUTE.

Every one of these defects may be practised safely from the maintenance plate: throw the matching TEST toggle, work the recovery above, and confirm the window extinguishes.

Specifications

Supply 415 V, 3-phase, 50 c/s, from battery M-G set
Bus limits Normal 415 V · compute floor 380 V · red sector below 350 V
Gyroscope Air-damped, 20-second run-up to ready; cage hold 3 s minimum
Tracking errors allowed ±0.8° in bearing · ±90 yd in range, for a matched sight
Range scale 800 to 16,000 yards
Time of flight assumed Predicted range divided by 866 yd/s, iterated thrice
Quadrant elevation 0.4° to 25° by range law
Barrel heat +16 units per salvo · cooling 1.15 units/s · interlock at 100 · liner ruin at 118
Rounds per engagement As ordered; the drums do not ration you, the heat gauge does
Director weight 6½ cwt, shock-mounted
Servicing Cams sealed — DO NOT OIL. Annual strip by armourer only.
