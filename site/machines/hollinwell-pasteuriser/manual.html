Overview

This panel governs the Line A high-temperature short-time (HTST) pasteuriser: a balance tank of raw chilled milk, a feed pump, a regeneration set, a hot-water heater section, a legal holding tube of 84.5 litres, and a fail-safe flow-diversion valve (the FDV). Milk leaves for the fillers only when it has held at or above 71.7 °C for not less than fifteen seconds. The FDV enforces that arithmetic without your help: anything under temperature, or moving too fast to hold its fifteen seconds, is turned back over the divert line into the balance tank.

The Panelman's job is therefore not to make milk safe — the valve does that — but to keep the plant running forward: keep the tank fed, keep the hot water up, keep the plates clean, and answer every lamp before it costs a vat. The panel was built and wired by the Hollinwell engineering department; treat it accordingly. The circular chart is a legal record: do not run with the drive off.

Controls

Control Kind Positions / range Action
MODE SELECTOR rotary switch, plum knob OFF · WASH · SANITISE · PRODUCE OFF isolates the pump and shuts steam to the water set. WASH circulates caustic for cleaning the plate pack — fouling clears only above 70 °C wash water. SANITISE recirculates water at 82 °C. PRODUCE draws raw milk from the tank through the plant to the fillers.
PUMP SPEED notched lever, 0–10 each notch ≈ 2 000 L/h; notch 10 = 20 000 L/h Sets the feed pump. Residence in the holding tube falls as speed rises: at notch 10 the tube holds 15.2 s — barely legal — so never raise speed with the hot water down. Below about 6 % tank level the pump starves and will trip.
HOT WATER SET knob, 62–85 °C setpoint of the hot-water set Water-set temperature. 76.5–78 °C carries notch 8 comfortably; higher speeds need more. Above 86 °C the HOT WATER HIGH lamp burns, and holding above 89 °C for twenty seconds trips the water set until the knob is brought below 85 °C again.
DIVERT TEST LEVER guarded lever, lead seal FORWARD (up) — DIVERT (held down) Hold to stroke the FDV to DIVERT against its solenoid; release and the weight must return it FORWARD inside three seconds. This proves the spring return, and one full stroke-and-return also frees a sticking seat. Break the seal only on a written order.
ALARM ACK mushroom push — Steadies any burning annunciator window you have seen. It silences nothing that is still true: a cleared condition extinguishes; an uncleared one stays lit, steady.
CHART DRIVE toggle ON / OFF Turns the circular chart and its clock. Pen 1 (red) records pasteurisation temperature, pen 2 (blue) records flow. Leave ON whenever the plant is running.
RESET red mushroom, collared — Returns the panel cold: pump stopped, mode OFF, every alarm, fault and trip cleared. For starting over after a shutdown — not for dodging an alarm you dislike.
MAINTENANCE flap hinged flap over test toggles FOUL PLATE PACK · STICK FDV SEAT · EXERCISE VALVE Test injection for trade tests and training. The left toggle fouls the plate pack; the right sticks the FDV seat; EXERCISE VALVE strokes the divert-test lever for you. Switch a toggle out again when the test is done.

Normal operation

  1. Panel cold: MODE SELECTOR at OFF, PUMP SPEED at 0, no windows burning. Switch CHART DRIVE ON and check both pens write and the chart clock runs.
  2. MODE SELECTOR to SANITISE. Raise HOT WATER SET toward 82 °C and let the water set come up. Circulate not less than two minutes.
  3. Check the balance tank sight glass: the float should hold level near four-fifths glass. Prove the FDV with one stroke on the DIVERT TEST LEVER — press, release, watch it come back smartly.
  4. MODE SELECTOR to PRODUCE. Advance PUMP SPEED notch by notch to 8. Watch TIC·1: pasteurisation temperature must settle at 72 °C or better, and the FDV node on the mimic must show FORWARD with the green lens lit.
  5. Trim HOT WATER SET (76.5–78 °C serves notch 8) until TIC·1 holds steady above 71.8 °C. Check FIC·2 reads the speed you asked for and RESIDENCE shows better than fifteen seconds.
  6. Watch the tank. At notch 8 the raw supply keeps pace; at notch 10 it does not — expect LEVEL LOW long before the glass empties, and ease off in time.
  7. To finish: take PUMP SPEED to 0, MODE SELECTOR to SANITISE for two minutes, then to OFF. Leave CHART DRIVE ON until the pens run off the record, then sign the chart.

Alarms

Window Colour Meaning Do this
PAST. TEMP LOW red Milk at the valve is under 71.6 °C. The FDV has already diverted — the plant is safe, the product is not going forward. Ease PUMP SPEED one notch or raise HOT WATER SET. When TIC·1 recovers past 72 °C the valve returns FORWARD by itself. Expect the tank to warm while the divert runs — see TANK OVERHEAT.
RESIDENCE SHORT red Flow is fast enough that the holding tube gives under fifteen seconds. Diverted. Reduce PUMP SPEED until RESIDENCE reads above 15 s. Never defeat this by raising temperature alone — the chart would show an illegal hold.
TANK LEVEL LOW amber Balance tank below 18 %. At about 6 % the pump begins to starve. Check the raw supply; reduce PUMP SPEED until the float recovers the level above 20 %.
TANK OVERHEAT red Diverted hot milk has warmed the vat past 24 °C. Past 31 °C the raw milk is spoiled: the panel latches spoilage, forces OFF and refuses PRODUCE. Stop the diverting early (fix temperature or speed). Once latched, the only way back is drain, refill and a full hot WASH — see Faults and recovery.
HOT WATER HIGH amber Water set above 86 °C. Twenty seconds over 89 °C trips the set off. Bring HOT WATER SET below 85 °C. After a trip, wait for the water to fall under 87 °C; the set relights once the knob is honest.
OUTLET WARM amber Milk leaving the cooler is over 6.5 °C — usually warm milk returned by a long divert has loaded the cooling section. Recover temperature first; the cooler follows. If it persists at low speed, have the ice-water supply checked.
MAINTENANCE FAULT amber A test injection is switched in at the maintenance flap. Finish your test and switch the toggle out. The plant behind it may still need the recovery procedure below.
FLOW TRIP red The pump ran starved more than six seconds and tripped to protect itself. Restore the level above 12 %; the trip releases itself once suction is proven. Restart per Normal operation from step 4.

Faults and recovery

Plate pack fouling

What you see. TIC·1 sags even with an honest water set; the regen figure on the mimic falls away; the FDV cycles to DIVERT and PAST. TEMP LOW burns, with product lost down the divert line on every cycle. Trade tests may inject this state from the MAINTENANCE flap.

Recovery. The plates are blinded and no amount of knob will carry them:

  1. Take PUMP SPEED to 0 and MODE SELECTOR to WASH.
  2. Raise HOT WATER SET so the wash water stands at 75 °C or better — caustic under 70 °C cleans nothing.
  3. Circulate. Watch the regen figure climb; allow not less than three minutes of hot circulation for a working set.
  4. Return via SANITISE two minutes, then PRODUCE per Normal operation step 4.

FDV seat sticking

What you see. The valve refuses its order: the mimic node holds whatever position it had, the FDV caption reads STUCK, and the line lamps lie about the true state of the milk. Worst case is FORWARD while under temperature — the green lens burns over illegal milk. Trade tests may inject this from the MAINTENANCE flap.

Recovery. The seat wants exercising, not nursing:

  1. Take PUMP SPEED to 0 so there is nothing passing through the valve.
  2. Give the DIVERT TEST LEVER one full stroke: press to DIVERT, hold a moment, release, and watch the spindle come back smartly. One stroke-and-return frees the seat; the STUCK caption clears when it moves again.
  3. If you cannot reach the lever, the EXERCISE VALVE push inside the maintenance flap strokes it for you.
  4. Prove it before returning product: run PRODUCE, chill the water set until you see a deliberate DIVERT, raise it again and watch the valve return FORWARD.

Raw spoilage latch — the cost of ignoring TANK OVERHEAT

What you see. Mode forced to OFF and selecting PRODUCE snaps back out again. The vat smells of cream cooked on a radiator, which is what it is.

Recovery. No shortcut exists: drain and refill the tank, then run a full hot WASH — three minutes above 70 °C — to satisfy the latch before PRODUCE is released. Enter the loss in the waste book with the cause.
