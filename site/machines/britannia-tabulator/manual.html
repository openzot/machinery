Overview

The Meridian IV reads punched cards at one hundred and fifty a minute, adds their figures into four eight-wheel counters, and prints a listing on the paper tape at the right-hand side of the cabinet. In this bureau it totals the quarter's rate demands: each card carries one hereditament, its rateable value in column twelve to seventeen, and its water charge in column forty to forty-five. Group breaks fall every twenty cards, and a final total prints when the run is stopped.

The job is defined on the plugboard by removable wires, exactly as the program chart beside the board directs. A machine which is not wired to its program feeds cards but prints nothing and accumulates nothing: the PRINT ENABLED lamp tells you when the board agrees with the chart. The machine is otherwise honest iron. It jams if you starve it of vacuum, it misreads if the brushes drift, and its counters lose carries when a carry pawl wears — every such condition announces itself on the check lamps along the top rail, and each has its remedy below. Ignore a check lamp and the machine does not stop being honest: torn cards, suspect figures and tripped breakers are how it tells you again.

Controls

Control Kind Positions and action
MAINS BREAKER guarded toggle ON / OFF under a hinged cover. isolates the machine entirely. after an overload trip it must not be re-closed until the motor cools below 62°C.
MOTOR STARTER pushbutton latches the contactor and brings the motor up to 1,440 r.p.m. dead machine refuses nothing else until she is up to speed.
FEED CLUTCH three-position lever RELEASE (feed disengaged), SINGLE (one card per engagement), RUN (continuous feed). arrows on the keyboard work her as well as the hand.
PLUGBOARD sixteen removable-wire hubs seat a wire by choosing its two hubs; choose a hub twice or a wired hub again to lift the wire. wires may not run within one column. program per the chart: CYCLE TAKE-OFF→PRINTER, FIELD A→CNT 1, FIELD B→CNT 2, GROUP BREAK→COMPARE.
COUNTER SELECTOR rotary, four positions selects CNT 1 RATES / CNT 2 WATER / CNT 3 ACCOUNTS / CNT 4 GROUPS. also fixes which counter a CARRY CHECK test refers to.
PAPER FEED pushbutton advances the carriage one line without printing.
STOP mushroom first press releases the clutch; second press drops the contactor and lets the motor coast.
CLEAR FEEDWAY warning lever-button clears a jammed or seized feedway. effective only with the clutch at RELEASE.
BRUSH ADJUST knob, twenty points re-registers the reading brushes. watch the read-check meter in the feedway panel while turning.
ENGINEER KEY keyed switch, spring return zero-sets all counters, resets the carry latches, cleans the air filter estimate, and threads a fresh deck when the hopper stands empty.
FAULT TEST SWITCHES toggle bank in engineer's drawer JAM / MISREAD / CARRY — force each condition for instruction and test. switching off does not clear a real jam; follow the recovery clauses.
LAMPS TEST pushbutton lights every lamp on the rail to prove the filaments.
SOUND CUT toggle silences the motor and card-feed sound reproduction.

Normal operation

  1. Walk the cabinet before power: hopper deck seated face-down, stacker empty, tape roll genuine, feedway visibly clear of fragments.
  2. Lift the guard and close the MAINS BREAKER. The MAINS ON lamp shows.
  3. Press MOTOR STARTER once and let her come up. Wait for the MOTOR lamp; do not engage the feed before it shows.
  4. Wire the board to JOB 41-R exactly per the program chart. When the last wire seats, the PRINT ENABLED lamp lights. A blank board wastes the whole deck silently.
  5. Turn the COUNTER SELECTOR to CNT 1 — RATES and confirm all four counter windows read zero. If they do not, switch the ENGINEER KEY before beginning.
  6. Move the FEED CLUTCH to SINGLE and let one card through. Watch her read: the read-check meter should stand above ninety and the first listing line print true.
  7. Move the FEED CLUTCH to RUN. She now takes one hundred and fifty cards a minute; group totals print in red every twenty cards.
  8. Mind the rail. Any check lamp demands the remedy in Alarms or Faults and recovery at once — a jam ignored tears cards until the feed seizes; brushes left out of register spoil the figures; heat left unattended trips the breaker.
  9. At the end of the quarter-deck move the clutch to RELEASE: the group remainder prints, and when the deck empties the FINAL TOTAL prints with the counters' verdict.
  10. To close down: STOP, MOTOR coasted, MAINS BREAKER off, guard closed. Lift the wires only when the board is to be re-programmed.

Alarms

Lamp Meaning Action
FEED JAM (ruby) a card has jammed in the feed throat. clutch to RELEASE, press CLEAR FEEDWAY. see clause F1.
READ CHECK (ruby) reading brushes out of register; digits are being misread. turn BRUSH ADJUST until the meter tops ninety and the lamp drops out. see clause F2.
CARRY CHECK (ruby) a counter is losing carries; totals short. note the selector, switch the ENGINEER KEY, re-run. see clause F3.
MOTOR OVERLOAD (ruby) motor temperature passed 88°C and the breaker has come out on overload. leave the MAINS BREAKER off; when the temperature falls below 62°C, close the breaker and restart with MOTOR STARTER.
MAINS ON (opal) breaker closed, machine live. —
MOTOR (opal) motor at speed; the feed may be engaged. —
PRINT ENABLED (opal) board agrees with the program chart. —

Faults and recovery

F1. Card jam in the feed throat

You see: FEED JAM on the rail, the red JAM flag over the throat, vacuum fallen, the listing frozen. If RUN is held, cards tear — the torn count climbs and after some seconds the feed seizes altogether.

To clear: bring the FEED CLUTCH to RELEASE (never clear a live feed). Press CLEAR FEEDWAY; the flag drops and the lamp goes out. Put the torn cards aside for the mending bench and re-thread from the hopper. The FAULT TEST SWITCH JAM forces this condition for instruction; the switch itself does not clear it — the lever-button does.

F2. Reading-brush misregister

You see: READ CHECK on the rail, the read-check meter sagging below ninety, and pounds, shillings and pence going quietly wrong into CNT 1; the final total will be stamped FIGURES SUSPECT.

To clear: turn BRUSH ADJUST slowly, a point at a time, watching the meter in the feedway panel. It climbs toward ninety-six and falls away either side of true registration; find the peak. When registration is exact the lamp drops out of itself. Figures accumulated while the check stood are suspect: switch the ENGINEER KEY to zero-set and run the batch again. The FAULT TEST SWITCH MISREAD forces the drift.

F3. Counter carry failure

You see: CARRY CHECK on the rail; the selected counter (per the COUNTER SELECTOR at the moment the fault arose) loses its thousands-carry and runs visibly short against the listing.

To clear: note the selector position, then switch the ENGINEER KEY. All counters zero, the carry latch re-seats and the lamp goes out. Thread a fresh deck with the key if the old one has run, set the selector to the counter the job calls for, and re-run. The FAULT TEST SWITCH CARRY forces the slip.
