Overview

This is the machine room console of the Högfors Isstadion municipal ice arena. Behind the wall there is 1800 m² of ice on a concrete slab; under the slab, ninety-six circuits of steel pipe in calcium-brine; out here, you and two R22 compressors. The plant's whole purpose is to hold the brine at −12 °C so that the ice stays at −7 °C, night after night, between hockey and public skating.

The room is built around one fact: frost. Moist air finds every cold surface, and the slab grows a blanket of it that strangles the cooling. The Nordkyl plant is therefore run as a rhythm — freeze hard, watch the frost line, give the slab hot gas between periods, and never let the brine run away while you do it. The panel tells you everything twice: once in glass and ink, once in sound.

R22 under pressure. Brine at minus twelve wets like any water and freezes like a grudge. If the horn sounds, silence it only after you have read why.

Controls

COMPRESSOR START
Green push. Starts the selected machine against its flow interlock. The start is blocked, and a note shown, if the brine pumps are not proving flow.
COMPRESSOR STOP
Red push. Unloads and stops the selected machine. Normal stop.
COMPRESSOR SELECTOR
Two-position rotary: No.1 / No.2. Selects which machine answers the START push. Turning it while running takes that machine off line — changeover is a stop, a select, and a fresh START.
CAPACITY LEVER
Notched slide lever, four steps: 25 · 50 · 75 · 100 %. Works the cylinder unloaders. Full capacity pulls hardest but loads the motor; drop a step or two once the brine reaches −12 °C. During defrost, work the lever down to 25 % to keep the discharge pressure civil.
BRINE PUMP SELECTOR
Three-position rotary: OFF · DUTY (one pump) · DUTY+AUX (both pumps). The plant may only run with flow proven. AUX also carries the loop if the duty pump trips.
DEFROST KEY
Key switch under a yellow-striped lift guard. NORMAL sends compressor discharge to the condenser; AVINNING (defrost) diverts hot gas through the slab instead, melting the frost. Watch the discharge gauge the whole time it is over.
RESURFACER CALL
Sends the ice resurfacer out: eight seconds to the door, forty-six seconds across the ice laying hot water, eight seconds home. The flood is a heat load — expect the brine to warm and the amps to move.
ALARM ACCEPT
Steadies every flashing annunciator window and silences the horn. It accepts nothing away: the window stays lit until the condition clears.
HORN CUT
Silences the horn only. Windows keep flashing until accepted.
LAMPS TEST
Hold down: every jewel, window and segment lights. Anything dark is a dead lamp — log it.
TRIP RESET
Clears the safety trip latch after the cause has been put right. It refuses to reset — and says so — while charge is still low or a pressure is still out of bounds.
TEST REFRIGERANT LEAK
Mechanic's test: simulates a leak on the suction line.
TEST BRINE FLOW LOSS
Mechanic's test: drops the flow proof as if the strainer bagged up.
TEST COMPRESSOR KNOCK
Mechanic's test: a developing mechanical knock on the running frame.
ISOLATE LEAK
Clamps the leaking section. First half of the leak repair.
TOP UP CHARGE
Feeds 16 kg of R22 from the charging cylinder into the receiver. Repeat until the sight glass runs full and the window goes dark.

Normal operation

  1. Lamps. Press and hold LAMPS TEST. Every window lit, both machines' jewels, the digital meter showing 8s. Release.
  2. Pumps first. Turn BRINE PUMP SELECTOR to DUTY. The PUMP A jewel lights and the flow dashes on the slab mimic begin to travel.
  3. Select the machine. COMPRESSOR SELECTOR to No.1 (No.2 is the standby frame).
  4. Start. Press COMPRESSOR START. The machine's jewel lights, the oil-pressure needle climbs off the pin, and suction begins to fall.
  5. Load her. Take CAPACITY LEVER to 100 % for pull-down while the hall fills. Watch motor amps stay under the red sector.
  6. Settle. As the digital meter passes about −12 °C, step CAPACITY LEVER back to 75 %. Brine should hold between −11 and −13 °C; suction around 1.5–2 bar; discharge below 10.
  7. Between periods: when FROST LIMIT lights — or before it does, roughly each hour of running — open the guard and turn the DEFROST KEY to AVINNING. Drop CAPACITY LEVER to 25 % first. Frost falls from the slab mimic inside two minutes; return the key to NORMAL and reload the lever.
  8. Flooding: after the Zamboni-shaped machine has been called and gone home, expect a wet band across the mimic and a short brine rise. Nothing is wrong. It recovers in a minute or two.
  9. Handover: leave the book open on the current shift's page. Note the hour-meter reading and anything the windows told you.

Alarms

Window Meaning What to do
LOW CHARGE Refrigerant has left the circuit; the charge glass bubbles. ISOLATE LEAK, then TOP UP CHARGE until the glass runs full.
LOW SUCTION Suction pressure under 1.15 bar with the machine running. Starved evaporator — usually the charge. Find the starvation. If LOW CHARGE shows, fix the charge; otherwise reduce capacity. Sustained, this trips the plant.
HIGH DISCHARGE Discharge over 13.4 bar — defrost left on too long or at too much capacity. CAPACITY LEVER to 25 % or take the key back to NORMAL. Sustained, this trips.
BRINE FLOW The flow proof has dropped: pumps dead or the strainer blocked. If pumping was intended, stop the pumps for five seconds and re-select DUTY — the restart surge lifts the debris. Start AUX if the duty pump is the trouble.
ICE TEMP HIGH Ice surface warmer than −2.8 °C: the ice is softening. Restore cooling — check flow, capacity and charge — and stay off the ice until it returns to −5 °C or colder.
FROST LIMIT Frost blanket past 10 mm; the slab is insulating itself. Run a defrost at the next break in play. Do not wait for ICE FAULT.
COMPRESSOR KNOCK Mechanical knock on the running frame; oil pressure sags with it. Select the other machine, then restart. A knocking frame must be inspected before it runs again.
MOTOR OVERLOAD Motor current above 66 A — knock, or abuse of the lever. Unload or swap machines. Above 72 A sustained, the plant trips itself.
ICE FAULT Ice quality lost below 42 %: soft, snow-covered, unplayable. Fix the cause — warmth or frost — let quality rebuild past 55 %, then TRIP RESET and refreeze.
SAFETY TRIP The plant has latched off: low suction, high discharge or overload held too long. Read the cause on the log, clear it, press TRIP RESET, restart per Normal operation.

Faults and recovery

refrigerant leak

You see: the charge sight glass filling with bubbles, LOW CHARGE within about twenty seconds, then LOW SUCTION as the evaporator starves; cooling fades though the machine sounds busy. Ignored, the plant trips on low suction.

Recovery: press ISOLATE LEAK to clamp the section. Then TOP UP CHARGE — five strokes of the cylinder brings the charge back above the alarm line. Let suction settle above 1.5 bar, press TRIP RESET, and restart per Normal operation. Recovery is complete when all windows are dark and the glass runs clear.

brine flow loss

You see: the flow dashes on the slab mimic stop, BRINE FLOW lights at once, and the brine begins to warm because nothing is being cooled. Ice temperature follows within minutes.

Recovery: turn BRINE PUMP SELECTOR to OFF and let it stand five seconds — the header tank settles the debris. Return it to DUTY; the restart surge lifts the strainer and flow is proved again. If the fault was a dead duty pump, DUTY+AUX brings the standby in instead. Accept the window when the dashes run.

compressor knock

You see: the running machine's jewel flickers, oil pressure sags toward 1.5 bar, motor amps pulse. COMPRESSOR KNOCK lights within ten seconds. Run it longer and the overload trip does what you should have done.

Recovery: turn COMPRESSOR SELECTOR to the other frame — the knocking machine comes off duty for inspection. TRIP RESET if the overload latched, press COMPRESSOR START, bring the lever back up in steps. Log the frame number for the mechanic.

Motor rating 55 kW each, 380 V 3~ 50 Hz, star-delta start
Nominal charge 340 kg R22 (receiver sized for full hold-up)
Brine Calcium chloride, 24 m³ loop, duty + standby centrifugal pumps
Brine design temps supply −12 °C nominal, alarm window −11 … −13 °C, limits −16.5 … +12 °C
Suction, normal 1.4 – 2.0 bar(g); alarm < 1.15 bar; trip < 0.95 bar held 25 s
Discharge, normal 8.5 – 10 bar(g); alarm > 13.4 bar; trip > 14.8 bar held 20 s
Oil pressure 3.3 – 3.7 bar running; below 2 bar indicates distress
Motor current 46 A at 75 % capacity, 58 A full; alarm > 66 A, trip > 72 A held 22 s
Slab 1800 m², 40 mm ice, surface −7 °C design
Frost policy hot-gas defrost between periods; alarm at 10 mm; ice damage beyond ~14 mm
Defrost discipline capacity 25 % during defrost; discharge checked continuously
Resurfacer event 62 s cycle, ≈400 l hot water, brine load ≈52 kW during pass
Panel supply 380/220 V, control 24 V DC, jewels 24 V incandescent
