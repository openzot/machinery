Overview

Creag Dhubh impounds the Dubh Water behind a concrete gravity dam and delivers it to a single vertical Francis set through a low-level tunnel, a surge shaft and 900 yards of 6-foot penstock. The machine drives a 16-pole generator at 375 rev/min and hands its power to the 33 kV board through Transformer No. 1. This bench works the whole chain from water to wire. The painted cutaway along its top is not decoration: loch level, surge shaft, penstock flow and the turning runner are all live, and the instruments beneath repeat them in brass and ivory.

The set is started by hand, synchronised by eye on the synchroscope, and left to the governor once on load. Remember that water is patient and heavy: move valves slowly, close them slower, and never ask the surge shaft to take more than it must. A careless hand hurts the penstock long before any lamp lights.

Controls

Control Type Action and positions
MAIN INLET VALVE Ratchet lever One stroke, one click of five. SHUT 000 to OPEN 100 in twenty clicks; the valve takes some forty-five seconds to follow the drum. Closing strokes order the valve shut the same way.
GOVERNOR CHANGE-OVER Three-position selector OFF releases the gate, which falls shut on its weight. HAND follows the SPEED CHANGER slowly. AUTO holds fifty hertz before synchronising and follows the SPEED CHANGER on load.
SPEED CHANGER Milled handwheel Sets GATE COMMAND, 000–100 on the counter. In HAND it is the gate itself; in AUTO it is the load.
EXCITATION Field rheostat knob FIELD current 0–10 amperes. Seven amperes gives eleven kilovolts at no load. On the bus it governs reactive megavars.
GENERATOR BREAKER Guarded rotary control Lift the yellow GUARD, then turn to CLOSE. The handle springs back to N. Turn to OPEN to take the set off the bus.
BEARING WATER PUMP Toggle ON circulates cooling water to the guide and thrust bearings from the tailrace intake. OFF leaves a trickle only.
ALARM ACCEPT Pushbutton Stops the flashing of a newly-lit tile. The lamp stays lit until the condition clears.
LAMPS TEST Pushbutton Lights every annunciator tile, pilot and jewel at once. Prove it before you trust a dark panel.
FAULT TEST SWITCHES Lidded test bank Under the lid: GOVERNOR OIL drains the accumulator as a leak; COOLING WATER cuts the bearing water supply; LOAD REJECT opens the breaker at full load. Return each switch to normal after test.
UNIT RESET Pushbutton Clears trip lockouts and the annunciator after a shutdown and restores the bench to cold positions. Use only when the levers are back where the cold sheet says.

Normal operation

  1. Cold sheet: MAIN INLET VALVE 000, GOVERNOR CHANGE-OVER OFF, SPEED CHANGER 000, EXCITATION zero, GENERATOR BREAKER open and guarded, BEARING WATER PUMP off. AUX SUPPLY pilot burning white.
  2. Press LAMPS TEST and satisfy yourself every lamp answers.
  3. Switch BEARING WATER PUMP ON. The BRG WATER LOW tile must stay dark.
  4. Stroke MAIN INLET VALVE up to 100, click by click. Watch PENSTOCK PRESSURE climb to about 115 metres and the SURGE SHAFT settle near its mark.
  5. Put GOVERNOR CHANGE-OVER to HAND.
  6. Raise the SPEED CHANGER steadily. The unit rolls; bring the large meter to 375 rev/min — the HERTZ needle standing on fifty.
  7. Bring EXCITATION to 7.0 amperes; MACHINE VOLTS rises to 11.0 and holds inside its green sector.
  8. Put GOVERNOR CHANGE-OVER to AUTO. The HERTZ needle steadies; the SERVO jewel burns green while oil pressure exceeds nine bar.
  9. Watch the SYNCHROSCOPE: the SLOW and FAST lamps tell you which way she is drifting. As the pointer slows towards twelve o'clock and the IN PHASE lamp burns, lift the GENERATOR BREAKER guard and turn to CLOSE as the pointer crosses twelve. CLOSED shows red, the ON LOAD pilot shows green. A closing made off the window strikes INCORRECT SYNC and the breaker refuses.
  10. Raise the SPEED CHANGER for eight to ten megawatts. Keep FOREBAY LEVEL above four metres, GUIDE BEARING below sixty-eight degrees, and GOVERNOR OIL between eighteen and twenty-one bar.
  11. To shut down: unload to zero, open the GENERATOR BREAKER, put the governor to OFF, let the gates fall, close MAIN INLET VALVE by strokes, stop the BEARING WATER PUMP last.

Alarms

Tile Meaning Immediate duty
FOREBAY LOW Forebay below 3.4 m; the governor caps the gate at 45 against air-drawing vortices. Reduce load. Below 2.0 m the intake trips the machine outright.
SURGE HIGH Surge shaft more than +2.2 m after a brisk gate movement. Touch nothing for two minutes and let the mass oscillate die. At +3.3 m the overpressure trip acts.
GOVERNOR OIL LOW Accumulator below 16 bar. Find the leak. Below 9 bar the AUTO servo is dead — change to HAND and hold the gate yourself.
BRG WATER LOW Cooling water supply lost or pump stopped. Restore supply at once; the bearing climbs towards 130 °C without it.
GUIDE BRG HOT Bearing metal at 68 °C or worse. Verify water flow, ease the load. The temperature trip acts at 88 °C.
OVERSPEED Speed past 112 per cent. Let the governor slam the gate; the mechanical trip acts at 140 per cent and wrecks the run.
LOAD REJECTION The breaker has opened on load. The unit races and settles; resynchronise before reloading.
INCORRECT SYNC A closing was attempted outside the window. The breaker refuses. Re-trim speed and volts, wait for IN PHASE, try again.

Faults and recovery

Governor oil pressure loss

You see: the GOVERNOR OIL LOW tile flashing; the oil gauge falling half a bar a second; AUTO going lazy and then dead below nine bar. This is the fault the FAULT TEST SWITCHES bank imitates with its GOVERNOR OIL switch.

To clear: put GOVERNOR CHANGE-OVER to HAND so the servo stops drinking; isolate the cause by returning the GOVERNOR OIL test switch to normal; hold the gate steady by hand where the load wants it. The pump recharges the accumulator; when the gauge passes seventeen bar the tile goes dark and you may return to AUTO. If the machine tripped meanwhile, let her settle, press UNIT RESET and begin again from the cold sheet.

Bearing cooling water loss

You see: BRG WATER LOW immediately; GUIDE BRG HOT within the minute on load; the temperature needle walking towards the red sector. Imitated by the COOLING WATER test switch, or simply by running with the BEARING WATER PUMP off.

To clear: restore the supply — return COOLING WATER to normal or start the BEARING WATER PUMP — and the bearing cools; the tile clears below sixty-eight degrees. If the eighty-eight degree trip has acted, wait until the needle falls under seventy, press UNIT RESET, and restart by the Normal operation sheet. Running her hot twice bends the shaft.

Load rejection

You see: a bang along the penstock, the surge column leaping, LOAD REJECTION lit, the breaker OPEN and the unit racing with OVERSPEED close behind if the governor is weak. Imitated by the LOAD REJECT test push, best tried at eight megawatts.

To recover: leave the governor alone — in AUTO with oil above nine bar it slams the gate itself, else close it by HAND. When the HERTZ needle is back on fifty, trim volts, watch IN PHASE, and reclose the GENERATOR BREAKER as before. The tile clears when the breaker closes below 105 per cent speed; reload gently and mind the surge shaft on the way.
