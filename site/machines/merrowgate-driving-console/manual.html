AMENDMENT No. 3 MAR 1974

Overview

This console drives a four-car 1973 Stock multiple unit over the Merrowgate Deep Line — a closed loop of nine point six kilometres with eight stations, signalled at six hundred metre intervals and fed at six hundred and thirty volts direct current. Traction is by four axle-hung motors controlled through a camshaft: the notches you select with the MASTER CONTROLLER cut resistance in steps, which is why the ammeter swings and the line volts dip as she takes current. Braking is air throughout, drawn from a MAIN RESERVOIR charged by the compressor you start with the AUXILIARIES switch. Doors are proved before power is offered, the track ahead is protected by trainstops, and the car protects you with the DEAD-MAN GRIP: hold her alive, ease off when demanded, and the train will stop itself if you do not.

The console was built by the Board's own Wolverton works. It is painted, stencilled and lit to be read at a glance in tunnel darkness. Treat it as it treats you: plainly.

Controls

Control Kind Positions and use
MASTER CONTROLLER Notched lever Twelve detents. Power: P1 gentle to P4 full. OFF between. Brake: B1 light to B6 firm. EM applies the emergency without regard to anything else. Drag the red knob or click a legend; arrow keys work when the knob is focused.
DEAD-MAN GRIP Sprung grip, held Squeeze and hold whenever the desk is live. Every forty seconds or so the collar flashes amber: ease off within five seconds and re-grip to prove you are still there. Ignore it, or let go entirely, and the emergency brake applies itself.
DOOR SELECTOR Rotary, three position LEFT for left-hand platform doors, RIGHT for right-hand, OFF between stations. Moving from OFF with doors open closes them first.
DOOR OPEN Green pushbutton Opens the selected side. It answers only when the selector is away from OFF, the train is inside a platform and practically at rest.
DOOR CLOSE Black pushbutton Closes and proves the doors. Pressed again when already shut, it re-seats a doubtful proving circuit.
AUXILIARIES Paddle switch Live and dead side of the desk: lights the gauges, starts the compressor, arms the door circuits. Off leaves a cold cab.
ALARM ACCEPT Pushbutton Silences the buzzer for the alarms showing. The lamps stay lit until the cause is cleared.
LAMPS TEST Pushbutton Lights every indicator window at once. Any window that stays dark is a blown lamp: report it.
TRIPCOCK RESET Key under red guard Lift the guard, turn the key. Answers only with the train standing. Resets the tripcock after a trainstop strike.
LEAK ISOLATION COCK Quarter-turn cock OPEN in normal service. Turned to SHUT it isolates a reservoir leak so the compressor can hold pressure.
FAULT TEST SWITCHES Three switches behind a cover Workshop tests, labelled TEST LEAK, TEST INTERLOCK and TEST TRAINSTOP. Each reproduces one of the faults in this book so that you may rehearse its recovery. Close the cover before service.
CAB SOUND / DESK RESET Pushbuttons CAB SOUND brings up traction hum, compressor and buzzer after your first touch of the desk. DESK RESET returns the whole console to its cold condition without reloading.

Normal operation

  1. Confirm the desk cold: MASTER CONTROLLER at OFF, doors closed, all windows dark except none. Squeeze the DEAD-MAN GRIP and keep it held.
  2. Put AUXILIARIES on. The windows prove themselves in a blink, the gauges light, and the compressor runs until the MAIN RES gauge stands between 84 and 100 lb/in². The COMPRESSOR window goes out at the top of the charge.
  3. Read the desk: LINE VOLTS about 630, MOTORS at zero, DOORS CLOSED burning green. If any red window shows, stop and consult Alarms below.
  4. At a platform, put the DOOR SELECTOR to the platform side and press DOOR OPEN. Give the passengers twenty seconds; the TIME LOST counter begins when you dawdle beyond that.
  5. Press DOOR CLOSE. Wait for the INTERLOCK window to go dark and DOORS CLOSED to show green before touching the controller.
  6. Take P1, then P2 as the ammeter falls back. Ease to P3 on the straight. Watch LINE VOLTS sag with current — if it nears 430 the LINE VOLTS LOW window asks you to notch back.
  7. Run to the line limit of 45 miles per hour. The OVERSPEED window and buzzer at 47 are telling you the next signal may not forgive you.
  8. Read the signals through the windscreen: green means the block is clear, single amber warns that the signal beyond is at danger, red means stop before the signal — a trainstop arm stands beside the rail and the TRIPCOCK on our bogie will strike it above walking pace.
  9. Approach a station on B3, easing through B2 and B1 to stop at the marker. Selector, open, dwell, close, and away again — eight stations to the lap.
  10. To secure: MASTER CONTROLLER to OFF, doors closed, AUXILIARIES off. The desk goes cold and holds nothing against you.

Alarms

Window Meaning Action
DOORS CLOSED (green) All doors shut and proved. Traction is permitted. None. Its absence with the selector away from OFF means doors stand open or moving.
INTERLOCK (amber) Doors not proved; power withheld. Close them properly before expecting power.
COMPRESSOR (amber) Compressor motor running. Information. Should run only between 84 and 100 lb/in².
LINE VOLTS LOW (amber) Line below about 430 V under heavy current. Notch back one step; the substation will catch up.
OVERSPEED (amber) Above 50 miles per hour. Brake to the limit. A red met at this pace ends badly.
LOW MAIN RES (red) Main reservoir below 62 lb/in². Find the loss. If a leak: see Faults and recovery. Below 38 the air failure brake applies and will not release until charge is restored.
DEAD-MAN (amber, flashing) The grip demands you ease off and re-prove yourself. Release the grip and take it again within five seconds. Ignored, it turns red: the emergency has applied.
TRIPCOCK (red) The tripcock has struck a trainstop at a signal worked at danger. Emergency brake applied. Stop completely, then TRIPCOCK RESET under the guard.

Faults and recovery

main reservoir leak

You see: the MAIN RES needle sinking steadily though the compressor keeps starting; LOW MAIN RES at 62 lb/in²; below 38 the air failure brake drags her to a stand and holds her there.

To clear: turn the LEAK ISOLATION COCK to SHUT. The compressor will now win, charging through 84, at which the air failure brake releases itself. Take OFF, allow the charge to reach 100, then work normally. When convenient, report the leak; DESK RESET restores a sound system after depot attention.

door interlock failure

You see: the INTERLOCK window burning red with the doors shut and proved; the controller moves and nothing answers, because the proving circuit reports a door open somewhere it cannot see.

To clear: come to stand at a platform. Press DOOR CLOSE to shut them, wait for the slam, then press DOOR CLOSE a second time to re-seat the proving circuit. The INTERLOCK window goes dark and power returns with DOORS CLOSED showing green.

trainstop trip (SPAD)

You see: a clunk beneath the leading bogie, the TRIPCOCK window flashing red and the emergency brake applying without being asked. You have passed a signal worked at danger faster than walking pace, or the test switch has struck the arm for you.

To clear: stop completely. Lift the red guard and turn TRIPCOCK RESET. The brake releases at rest. Pass the signal thereafter at caution under the rule, and expect questions.

Specifications

Supply 630 V DC, shore substations, current collected per shoe
Traction Four axle-hung motors, camshaft control, P1–P4
Maximum current 380 A at P4 against a fresh line
Line voltage alarm 430 V under load
Service speed 45 mph limit; overspeed warned at 47, alarmed at 50
Main reservoir Compressor cut-in 84, cut-out 100 lb/in²
Air failure brake Applies at 38 lb/in², releases above 84 with charge restored
Brake cylinder, full 72 lb/in² at B6 or emergency
Emergency effort 265 kN against 190 kN at B6
Train mass 132 t tare; about 190 t with a full evening crowd
Vigilance cycle Grip demanded eased every 40 s; five seconds to obey
Dead-man grace Six seconds from a live desk to an unheld grip
Line 9.6 km loop, eight stations, signals every 600 m, platforms 110 m
Scheduled dwell 20 s; time lost counted beyond it
Console Type DM/73, Wolverton-built, set number 3541
